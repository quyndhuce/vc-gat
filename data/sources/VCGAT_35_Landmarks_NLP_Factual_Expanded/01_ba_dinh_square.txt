Ba Dinh Square (Quảng trường Ba Đình) is located in Ba Dinh district, Hanoi. It is a civic square and national memorial.
Ho Chi Minh read the Declaration of Independence at Ba Dinh on 2 September 1945.
The present monumental landscape reflects subsequent commemorative planning.
The square faces the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum.
The flagpole and broad open forecourt establish a ceremonial axis.
The Presidential Palace and One Pillar Pagoda are nearby but distinct sites.
Characteristic physical features include broad paved ceremonial square, lawns in geometric bands, flagpoles, and the monumental mausoleum facade.
Ba Dinh Square is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, ceremonial lawns, flagpole, adjacent Presidential Palace precinct.
The site is associated with national Day commemorations and civic ceremonies.
