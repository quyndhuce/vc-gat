Hanoi Museum (Bảo tàng Hà Nội) is located in Pham Hung Street, Nam Tu Liem district, Hanoi. It is a contemporary history museum.
The museum building opened around Hanoi's millennial celebrations in 2010 and presents the city's historical and cultural heritage.
The building's upper floors project outward above a narrower base.
Interior circulation and galleries occupy several levels around open space.
Characteristic physical features include inverted-pyramid massing, broad overhanging upper floors, glass surfaces, interior atrium and exhibition displays.
Hanoi Museum is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Inverted-pyramid building, entrance plaza, exhibition halls, atrium.
The site is associated with collections and exhibitions on Hanoi's archaeology, history and material culture.
