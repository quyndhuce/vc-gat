Vietnam Museum of Ethnology (Bảo tàng Dân tộc học Việt Nam) is located in Nguyen Van Huyen Street, Cau Giay district, Hanoi. It is a ethnographic museum.
The museum documents cultural practices and material heritage of Vietnam's ethnic communities.
It opened to the public in the 1990s.
Indoor exhibitions occupy the main museum building.
An outdoor architectural garden displays reconstructed or relocated ethnic house types in a landscaped setting.
Characteristic physical features include exhibition galleries, ethnographic artifacts, outdoor traditional houses, timber structures and gardens.
Vietnam Museum of Ethnology is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Main exhibition building, outdoor ethnic houses, garden paths, cultural artifacts.
The site is associated with ethnographic interpretation, cultural diversity and traditional architecture.
The Vietnam Museum of Ethnology is located in Cau Giay district, Hanoi.
The museum presents the cultural diversity of Vietnam's ethnic groups.
The main indoor exhibition building is known as the Trong Dong Building.
The museum includes an outdoor architectural exhibition.
The outdoor exhibition contains full-scale traditional house structures.
Some displayed houses stand on stilts.
Elevated houses use posts to support their living floors.
Stairs connect the ground with raised living areas.
Roof shapes differ among the displayed house types.
The outdoor exhibits represent architectural traditions from different ethnic communities.
The museum also presents clothing, tools, ritual objects, and photographs.
Exhibition labels provide cultural and historical explanations.
The museum grounds contain walking paths between architectural exhibits.
