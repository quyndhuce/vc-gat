Lake Thien Quang (Hồ Thiền Quang) is located in Hai Ba Trung district, Hanoi. It is a urban lake and cultural landscape.
Thien Quang Lake is an established central Hanoi landscape framed by streets, residential blocks and nearby religious sites.
A shoreline path follows the lake edge.
Thong Nhat Park lies nearby but is a separate park complex.
Characteristic physical features include open water, lakeside trees, paths, benches and city traffic.
Lake Thien Quang is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Lake shore, walking paths, roadside trees.
The site is associated with urban leisure, walking and neighborhood social life.
Thien Quang Lake is situated in central Hanoi.
The lake is bordered by streets and urban buildings.
The water surface occupies the center of the landscape.
Sidewalks and planted areas occur along the shoreline.
The lake is located near Thong Nhat Park.
The lake is distinct from Bay Mau Lake inside Thong Nhat Park.
Reflections of trees and buildings are visible on the lake under suitable conditions.
The lake provides an open visual space within a dense urban district.
Public paths and roads connect the lakeshore with nearby neighborhoods.
