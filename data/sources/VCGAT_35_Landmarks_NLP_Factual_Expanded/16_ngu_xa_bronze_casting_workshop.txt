Ngu Xa Bronze Casting Workshop (Làng nghề đúc đồng Ngũ Xã) is located in Ngu Xa area, Truc Bach peninsula, Ba Dinh district, Hanoi. It is a traditional metalworking heritage.
Ngu Xa is historically associated with skilled bronze casting and religious metalwork.
The craft is part of Hanoi's artisan history.
The historic craft quarter lies on the Truc Bach peninsula.
Workshops, religious buildings and residential streets are distinct spaces.
Characteristic physical features include bronze statues, bells, metal vessels, workshop tools and casting displays where present.
Ngu Xa Bronze Casting Workshop is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Bronze artifacts, casting tools, craft streets, local temples.
The site is associated with casting, finishing, polishing and transmission of artisan skills.
Ngu Xa is a traditional bronze-casting area near Truc Bach Lake.
Ngu Xa artisans historically produced bronze religious objects and statues.
Bronze casting requires preparing a mold.
Molten metal is poured into a prepared mold.
The metal cools and solidifies inside the mold.
The cast object is removed from the mold.
Artisans refine the surface of the object after casting.
Bronze objects can be polished or decorated.
