Yen So park (Công viên Yên Sở) is located in Hoang Mai district, Hanoi, near Gamuda City. It is a modern ecological urban park.
Yen So Park is a large contemporary green-space project associated with environmental landscape improvements in southern Hanoi.
Multiple lakes and planted areas are connected by walking routes.
The park adjoins urban development but is distinct from individual residential projects.
Characteristic physical features include broad lakes, planted lawns, pedestrian paths, bridges, pavilions and open green space.
