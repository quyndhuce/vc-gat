Independence Palace (Dinh Độc Lập) is located in Nam Ky Khoi Nghia Street, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City. It is a modernist presidential palace and historical museum.
The current palace was built in the 1960s to designs by architect Ngo Viet Thu.
It is associated with the events of 30 April 1975.
A central lawn and driveway front the palace.
Interior reception and meeting rooms connect through corridors.
Service and bunker spaces occupy other levels.
Characteristic physical features include modernist facade, vertical screens, broad lawn, ceremonial rooms, rooftop and historic vehicles.
