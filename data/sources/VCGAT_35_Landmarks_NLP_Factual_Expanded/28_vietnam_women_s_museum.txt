Vietnam Women's Museum (Bảo tàng Phụ nữ Việt Nam) is located in Ly Thuong Kiet Street, Hoan Kiem district, Hanoi. It is a thematic cultural museum.
The museum presents women's experiences in Vietnamese family, history, work and cultural life.
Exhibition spaces are arranged within a multistory urban museum building.
Galleries cover different thematic areas.
Characteristic physical features include modern museum entrance, interior galleries, clothing, textiles, photographs and everyday objects.
Vietnam Women's Museum is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Museum facade, thematic galleries, textile and costume displays.
The site is associated with women's histories, family life, fashion and social participation.
The Vietnamese Women's Museum is located on Ly Thuong Kiet Street in Hanoi.
The museum presents the history and cultural roles of Vietnamese women.
The museum was established under the Vietnam Women's Union.
Exhibition topics include family life, historical participation, and women's fashion.
The museum displays clothing and textile objects.
Traditional costumes represent different cultural communities.
Photographs and personal objects provide historical context.
Exhibition cases contain artifacts and explanatory labels.
The museum building contains multiple floors of exhibition space.
Visitors move between galleries using stairs and other circulation areas.
The museum is a cultural institution rather than a historic temple.
