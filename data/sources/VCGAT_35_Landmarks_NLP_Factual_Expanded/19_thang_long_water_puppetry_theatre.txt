Thang Long Water Puppetry Theatre (Nhà hát Múa rối Thăng Long) is located in Dinh Tien Hoang Street, near Hoan Kiem Lake, Hanoi. It is a traditional performing-arts venue.
The theater presents Vietnamese water puppetry, a performance tradition associated with northern rural communities.
The indoor water stage occupies the performance hall.
Audience seating faces the water surface and the concealed puppeteer operating area.
Characteristic physical features include water stage, puppet figures, curtain, musicians and theater facade.
