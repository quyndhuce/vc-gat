Lake Ba Mau (Hồ Ba Mẫu) is located in Dong Da district, Hanoi. It is a urban lake and neighborhood landscape.
Ba Mau Lake is an urban water body embedded in Hanoi's changing residential and green-space network.
The lake is a separate water body from Bay Mau Lake in Thong Nhat Park.
Pedestrian access and shoreline treatment vary around its perimeter.
Characteristic physical features include lake surface, paved shores, planted edges, paths and surrounding urban buildings.
Lake Ba Mau is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Lake shore, paths, planted edges.
The site is associated with everyday recreation and neighborhood landscape.
