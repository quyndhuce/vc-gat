Vietnam National Puppetry Theatre (Nhà hát Múa rối Quốc gia Việt Nam) is located in Truong Chinh Street area, Hanoi. It is a national performing-arts institution.
The national puppetry institution develops and presents Vietnamese puppetry traditions including water and stage puppetry.
Performance venues include a stage and audience area.
The exact arrangement depends on the production and venue.
Characteristic physical features include puppets, theatrical sets, performance stage, musicians and audience seating.
Vietnam National Puppetry Theatre is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Performance stage, puppet figures, props, musical ensemble.
The site is associated with puppetry performance, training and cultural preservation.
The Vietnam National Puppetry Theatre is a performing arts institution in Hanoi.
The theatre presents traditional Vietnamese puppetry.
Water puppetry uses a water stage and wooden puppet figures.
Puppeteers manipulate puppets through control mechanisms concealed from the audience.
Traditional music accompanies many performances.
Puppet characters represent villagers, animals, and legendary figures.
The theatre also presents other forms of puppetry.
The performance venue includes stage areas and audience seating.
The institution is distinct from Thang Long Water Puppetry Theatre near Hoan Kiem Lake.
The theatre contributes to the preservation and presentation of Vietnamese performing arts.
