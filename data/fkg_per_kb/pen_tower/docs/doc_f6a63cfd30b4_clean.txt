The Pen Tower, also known as Tháp Bút, stands majestically on a small mound on the northeastern side of Hoan Kiem Lake, its ancient and graceful appearance a testament to the rich cultural heritage of Vietnam. This five-story stone tower, built in the 18th year of Tự Đức (1865), is a masterpiece of Confucian scholar Nguyễn Văn Siêu's design, situated outside the entrance to the Huc Bridge and Ngoc Son Temple.

The tower's unique architecture is characterized by its gradual narrowing of tiers toward the top, creating the illusion of a pen reaching upward, as if writing in the sky. The pen-shaped tip at the top, pointing straight upward, symbolizes the spirit of learning and the literary tradition of the Vietnamese people. The three Chinese characters "Ta Thanh Thien" engraved at the base of the tower, meaning "to write on the blue sky," further enhance its symbolic significance.

The Pen Tower's surroundings are a tranquil oasis, with the calm water of the lake reflecting the structure's image on its surface. Rows of green trees provide shade and create a harmonious setting between nature and architecture. As the light changes throughout the day, the Pen Tower takes on different moods, appearing radiant under sunlight and contemplative at dusk, contributing to the unique beauty of Hoan Kiem Lake.

The tower's construction is deeply rooted in Vietnamese history, commemorating the military achievements of Lord Trịnh in suppressing the rebellion of Quận Hẻo Nguyễn Danh Phương. The mound on which the tower stands symbolizes the mountain named Độc Tôn, which was built to commemorate the victory. The tower's architectural complex celebrates literature, but also honors the military achievements of Lord Trịnh.

The surrounding area is steeped in historical significance, with the Inkstone Pavilion located at the beginning of the Huc Bridge. The inkstone, carved in the shape of a half-peach, is a symbol of the connection between the tower and the lake. The inscription on the inkstone, written by Nguyễn Văn Siêu, is a profound reflection on the relationship between the tower and the lake, as well as the connection between the past and the present.

Today, the Pen Tower remains an iconic landmark, attracting tourists and locals alike who come to admire its beauty and significance. The surrounding area is a popular spot for photography, with the tower's unique architecture and the tranquil lake creating a breathtaking backdrop. The Pen Tower is a testament to the rich cultural heritage of Vietnam, a symbol of the country's literary and historical traditions, and a reminder of the importance of preserving cultural heritage for future generations.
