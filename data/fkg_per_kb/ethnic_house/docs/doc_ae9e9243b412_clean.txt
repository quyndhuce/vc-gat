In the heart of Hanoi, Vietnam's capital city, lies the Museum of Ethnology, a cultural treasure trove that transports visitors to a tranquil miniature version of the country. The outdoor folk architecture exhibition area, known as the Ethnic House, is a serene oasis amidst the bustling city, featuring a collection of traditional houses that showcase the authentic living spaces and cultural heritage of Vietnam's 54 ethnic groups. This sacred space was meticulously recreated by indigenous artisans who traveled from distant villages to the capital, bringing with them their ancient techniques and legends.

The architectural complex rises majestically, its soaring, axe-blade shaped nhà rông roof a striking feature that immediately catches the eye. The rustic brown hue of the wooden structure blends seamlessly with the worn thatched roof, evoking a sense of rustic charm. The unique staircase carved in the shape of a nipple, leaning against the porch wall, is a distinguishing feature that adds to the complex's visual appeal.

As visitors stroll through the tree-shaded garden and along the winding path, they are surrounded by the tranquil atmosphere of a bygone era. The rustic dwellings, scattered amidst the lush greenery, seem to whisper stories of the past, their wooden beams and rattan branches bearing the weight of history. The sound of gongs and the gentle rustling of fabric under the floorboards create a soothing melody that transports visitors to a world of serenity.

On Tet holidays, the Ethnic House comes alive with vibrant colors and sounds, as the air is filled with the sweet scent of incense and the soft glow of lanterns. The resounding beats of the gongs and the gentle swaying of the Xoe dancers under the canopy of festive flags create a festive atmosphere that is quintessentially Vietnamese. As visitors touch the cool bamboo wall, they are filled with a sense of connection to the land and its people, a feeling that is deeply rooted in the country's cultural heritage.

The Ethnic House is a testament to the country's rich cultural diversity, a celebration of the 54 ethnic groups that have lived together under the roof of the Fatherland. It is a place where tradition meets innovation, where the past and present converge, and where the cultural identity of Vietnam is proudly showcased.
