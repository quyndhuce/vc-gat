Tao Sach Pagoda, also known as Tao Sách or Ling Son Temple, is located at number 386 on Lac Long Quan Street, in Nhât Tan Ward, Tay Ho District, Hanoi. The pagoda belongs to the Tao Dong sect, which originated from Zen Master Thuy Nguyet, who passed down the teachings to disciples who became the abbots of various temples around West Lake.

In 1993, the pagoda was recognized by the state as a national historical and cultural relic. Besides being a place for Buddhist worship, Tao Sach Pagoda serves as the base for the Hoa Nghiem Association. The pagoda houses 42 couplets (39 in Classical Chinese and 3 in Nom script), 23 large inscriptions, two bells, one of which was cast in the third year of Emperor Minh Mang's reign (1822), and 24 steles, including twelve that were established during the reign of Emperor Bao Dai in 1941.

The current abbot of Tao Sach Pagoda is Venerable Thich Nguyen Hanh, born in 1958, who belongs to the Lam Te sect and the Die Xuyen lineage.
