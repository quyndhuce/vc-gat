In the heart of the historic Hanoi heritage complex, lies the majestic High Sanctuary, also known as Điện Đại Thành, a sacred space where the essence of Confucianism and royal learning converge. This architectural wonder, situated in the vast fourth courtyard, is a testament to the ingenuity of traditional Vietnamese court style, with its grand nine-bay hall supported by massive dark-brown lim wood columns and a solemn yin-yang tile roof. The High Sanctuary's tranquil atmosphere is palpable, as the scent of incense wafts through the air, carried by the gentle breeze.

As one approaches the complex, the imposing bronze incense burner, standing majestically in the center of the courtyard, commands attention. This sacred symbol of reverence and wisdom is a focal point for devotees, who come to pay homage to the revered Confucius, the "sage of all ages," alongside other esteemed sages. The High Sanctuary's name, "Đại Thành," is a testament to the crystallization of humanity's great truths, as it embodies the essence of Confucian teachings.

The High Sanctuary's history dates back to the early days of the relic, when it was established to worship Confucius, Tứ Phối, and other sages. Over time, it has become a revered spiritual touchpoint, where individuals from all walks of life, including scholars and pilgrims, come to seek wisdom and guidance. The tranquil surroundings, surrounded by the Tả vu and Hữu vu wings, create an atmosphere of serenity, allowing visitors to shed their worldly worries and connect with the profound spiritual energy that permeates this sacred space.

As one walks through the High Sanctuary, the intricate carvings and ornate decorations on the walls and columns come alive, telling the story of a rich cultural heritage. The soft glow of the incense smoke, carried by the gentle breeze, adds to the mystical ambiance, as the scent of sandalwood and other fragrances wafts through the air. The High Sanctuary is a place where tradition and reverence meet, where the past and present converge, and where the pursuit of knowledge and wisdom is celebrated.
