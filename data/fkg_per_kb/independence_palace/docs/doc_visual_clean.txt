Independence Palace is a landmark historical monument located on Nam Ky Khoi Nghia Street in District 1, Ho Chi Minh City. It is surrounded by a vast shaded park of ancient tropical trees and expansive front lawns with a circular fountain. The palace features a white facade with vertical concrete sun-shading louvers that evoke stylized bamboo stalks. The rooftop includes a pavilion and a central flagpole, forming the character Trung, which symbolizes loyalty. Inside, there are state banquet and presidential reception halls adorned with lacquer paintings, as well as underground war command rooms equipped with vintage maps and telecommunications equipment. The palace also has a rooftop helipad where a historic UH-1 helicopter is displayed.
