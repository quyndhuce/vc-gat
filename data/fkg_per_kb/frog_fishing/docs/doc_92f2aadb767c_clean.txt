In recent years, visitors to Côn Sơn have been captivated by a unique and enchanting spectacle known as frog fishing, a charming activity that has become a significant part of the local tourism scene. This peculiar tradition involves frogs performing acrobatic feats such as jumping through hoops, dancing to music, and engaging in playful competitions, all of which are designed to entertain both children and adults alike. The practice of training frogs to perform these tasks is a relatively new addition to the region's cultural offerings, having emerged as a novel attraction in response to the growing interest in local tourism.

Mr. Lê Văn Cang, a member of the Côn Sơn Agricultural Cooperative, has taken it upon himself to contribute to the enrichment of the area’s tourist offerings. His vision is to create a more diverse range of activities that can engage visitors during their stay at Côn Sơn, particularly during the summer season. Inspired by the natural agility and playfulness of frogs, Mr. Cang began experimenting with the idea of training them to perform various tricks, which eventually evolved into the popular frog fishing performances.

The process of training frogs to perform these intricate maneuvers requires meticulous attention to detail and patience. Mr. Cang carefully selects young frogs that exhibit qualities such as agility, responsiveness, and a certain level of curiosity. These chosen frogs are then subjected to a rigorous training regimen that includes conditioning them to respond to commands, learning to jump through hoops, and even dancing to music. It takes approximately 30 days for a frog to become proficient enough to perform these tasks reliably.

One of the most challenging aspects of this training is maintaining the vibrant colors of the frogs, which are crucial for the visual appeal of the performances. The frogs tend to lose their bright hues over time due to their natural tendency to adapt to their surroundings. Therefore, Mr. Cang must ensure that the frogs are kept in environments that preserve their colorful appearance, which adds another layer of complexity to the training process.

Visitors from various regions, including tourists from Nghệ An, have expressed great enthusiasm and delight in witnessing these performances. The sight of trained frogs leaping through hoops in a coordinated manner is truly mesmerizing. Each hoop is painted with vibrant colors and arranged in a sequence that encourages the frogs to compete and showcase their agility. The performance typically consists of six rounds, each round featuring different colored hoops that the frogs must navigate through.

During the performances, the frogs demonstrate remarkable coordination and flexibility, jumping through hoops of varying sizes and heights. The training sessions involve not only physical exercises but also auditory cues, ensuring that the frogs can respond accurately to musical rhythms and vocal commands. This synchronization between the frogs and the trainers creates a harmonious and entertaining display that captivates audiences of all ages.

As the summer approaches, Mr. Cang plans to expand the repertoire of frog performances, introducing new routines that cater specifically to the interests of children visiting Côn Sơn. These performances are designed to be educational and fun, providing a memorable experience that combines entertainment with a touch of cultural insight into the local fauna and traditions.

The success of these frog fishing performances has not only enhanced the appeal of Côn Sơn as a tourist destination but also contributed to the preservation and promotion of local culture. By showcasing the unique abilities of the frogs, Mr. Cang and his team have created an engaging and interactive experience that resonates with visitors, making their stay at Côn Sơn all the more memorable.
