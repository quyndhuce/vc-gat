The Walking Street around Hoàn Kiếm Lake is a pedestrian zone that includes 16 streets surrounding the lake, prohibiting vehicular traffic. The concept of a walking street was first tested in Hà Nội in 2004 along Hàng Đào and Đồng Xuân streets, and later expanded to the first-level preservation area in 2014, which included Hàng Buồm, Hàng Giầy, Lương Ngọc Quyến, Mã Mây, Đào Duy Từ, and Tạ Hiện streets. The current Walking Street around Hoàn Kiếm Lake officially began operations on September 1, 2016. It encompasses several streets such as Đinh Tiên Hoàng, Hàng Khay, Lê Thái Tổ (the half of Lê Thái Tổ road facing Hoàn Kiếm Lake from Hàng Trống to Hàng Khay), Lê Lai (from Đinh Tiên Hoàng to Lý Thái Tổ), Lê Thạch, Trần Nguyên Hãn (from Đinh Tiên Hoàng to Lý Thái Tổ), Đinh Lễ, Nguyễn Xí, Tràng Tiền (from the National Opera House to Hàng Bài), Lò Sũ (from Đinh Tiên Hoàng to Nguyễn Hữu Huân), Hàng Dầu (from Đinh Tiên Hoàng to the wooden bridge), Hồ Hoàn Kiếm, Lương Văn Can (from Hàng Hành to Lê Thái Tổ), Hàng Bài (from Hai Bà Trưng to Hàng Khay), and Bảo Khánh (from Ngõ Bảo Khánh to Lê Thái Tổ).

On December 31, 2020, Hà Nội further expanded the pedestrian zone to the southern part of the Old Quarter, connecting to the northern area of Hoàn Kiếm Lake. This expansion includes Đinh Liệt, Gia Ngư, Cầu Gỗ, Hàng Bè, Hàng Dầu, Hàng Bạc, Đào Duy Từ (from Chợ Gạo to Ô Quan Chưởng), Ô Quan Chưởng (including both Ô Quan Chưởng street and the intersection of Hàng Chiếu - Thanh Hà), and several alleys like Cầu Gỗ, Trung Yên, and Phất Lộc.

The Walking Street operates on three evenings each weekend, from Friday to Sunday. During summer, it runs from 7 PM to midnight, while during winter, it extends from 6 PM to midnight. Special events may extend these operating hours. Plans are also underway to expand the walking zones.

During the inauguration of the Walking Street, Mr. Nguyễn Đức Chung, Chairman of Hà Nội's People's Committee, stated, "We believe that with the unique cultural characteristics of Thăng Long land, combined with the convergence and adaptation of cultural and culinary activities to contemporary life, will meet the cultural and recreational needs of the people of Hà Nội and tourists both domestically and internationally."

While the Walking Street is operational, hotels rated three stars or higher, bars, and restaurants in the Hoàn Kiếm Lake area are allowed to operate until 2 AM. The Walking Street features automatic water dispensers and free Wi-Fi stations. It serves not only as a place for leisurely walks, socializing, and entertainment but also hosts various folk games and spontaneous or organized cultural and artistic performances. By October 2018, over two years since its establishment, the Hoàn Kiếm Lake and surrounding areas had hosted hundreds of cultural events, including 185 large-scale cultural events involving eight provinces and cities within Vietnam and 17 foreign countries.
