In the heart of Hanoi's Old Quarter, nestled on Đinh Tiên Hoàng Street, lies the enchanting Nhà hát múa rối Thăng Long, a poetic art space that has been captivating audiences since its inception in 1969. This heavenly dance performance, known as Múa Tiên, is a testament to the sophistication of Vietnamese art born from the wet rice civilization. As the name Thăng Long implies, the performance is deeply rooted in the city's rich history, dating back to the time when King Lý Thái Tổ moved the capital and beheld the rising dragon.

As you step into the theater, you are transported to a dreamlike realm, where the soft silk ribbons swirling rhythmically on the rippling waves create a mesmerizing spectacle. The vibrant peach-colored silk, highlighting the glossy lacquer on the wooden core, immediately catches the eye, drawing you into the enchanting world of fairy puppets. The rustic, hanging bamboo screen provides a serene backdrop, as the figures glide gently on the undulating water, their movements a testament to the virtuosity of the artisans.

As the music of the đàn tranh and flute blends in perfect harmony, the entire space transforms into a fairyland, transporting visitors from all over and residents of the capital to a world of ethereal beauty. The performance is not only a showcase of the artisans' skill but also a therapy to purify the soul, offering an invaluable experience that helps us find peace and tranquility amidst the hustle and bustle of urban life. As the music fades, what remains deep within everyone's mind is an otherworldly, ethereal sensation of extraordinary lightness, a reminder of the magic that lies within this enchanting performance.
