Lake Thien Quang, also known as Halais Lake, is a serene oasis nestled in the heart of Hanoi's bustling Hai Ba Trung district. This picturesque lake, with its tranquil waters and lush greenery, offers a peaceful retreat from the city’s hustle and bustle. The lake’s origins trace back to 1831 when it was referred to as "Ho Lien Thuy," covering a larger area than it does today. During the French colonial era, parts of the lake were filled in to accommodate urban development, shaping its current form. In 2003, significant renovations took place, including the reinforcement of its banks, water replacement, and the addition of floral gardens, benches, and other amenities, enhancing its appeal for visitors.

The name "Thien Quang" translates to "the light of compassion," reflecting a desire for peace within the urban landscape. The lake’s serene ambiance and natural beauty make it a popular spot for both locals and tourists seeking tranquility. It spans approximately 5 hectares, providing ample space for leisurely walks, cycling, and enjoying the fresh air. The lake’s perimeter measures around 1.6 kilometers, allowing visitors to take leisurely strolls or cycle around its entirety, taking in the scenic views and the soothing atmosphere.

Lake Thien Quang is surrounded by four tree-lined streets—Quang Trung, Nguyen Du, Tran Binh Trong, and Tran Nhan Tong—creating a verdant backdrop that enhances its charm. Adjacent to the lake is the main entrance to Thong Nhat Park, further enriching the area with recreational opportunities. The lake’s location makes it easily accessible, with various transportation options available, including personal vehicles, public buses, and ride-sharing services like Green SM, which offers eco-friendly electric vehicles.

Visitors can enjoy a variety of activities at Lake Thien Quang, each offering unique experiences. Morning and evening walks along the lakeside are particularly popular, with many residents and tourists choosing these times to unwind and appreciate the natural beauty. The early morning hours and late afternoon provide ideal conditions for a peaceful stroll or bike ride, allowing one to fully immerse in the serene environment. The cool breeze and the gentle rustling of leaves create a calming atmosphere, perfect for relaxation.

One of the most enchanting experiences at Lake Thien Quang is watching the swans glide gracefully across the water. These elegant birds, with their black and white feathers, add a touch of elegance and tranquility to the scene. The swans are released into the lake during weekends, creating a picturesque sight that captivates visitors. Observing these majestic creatures provides a momentary escape from daily stresses, fostering a sense of peace and serenity.

For those looking to combine relaxation with socializing, the cafes surrounding the lake offer a delightful setting. Establishments such as All Day Coffee, Cafe In, PALM Cafe, and Ancient Coffee provide cozy spaces where visitors can savor a cup of coffee while enjoying the serene views of the lake. The early morning or late afternoon, when the light is softer and more romantic, is the best time to visit these cafes, capturing the essence of the lake in a picturesque manner.

In addition to the lake itself, the nearby Tran Nhan Tong pedestrian street adds another layer of enjoyment. This vibrant area hosts a variety of activities, including outdoor games, music events, and cultural performances, making it a lively destination for families and young people. The street is lined with food stalls offering a range of local delicacies, allowing visitors to indulge in the flavors of Hanoi while enjoying the festive atmosphere.

Overall, Lake Thien Quang stands out as a haven of tranquility amidst the urban chaos of Hanoi. Its rich history, serene environment, and diverse recreational activities make it a must-visit destination for anyone seeking a peaceful respite from the city’s fast-paced life. Whether you are a resident of Hanoi or a tourist exploring the city, Lake Thien Quang offers a unique blend of natural beauty, cultural significance, and recreational opportunities, ensuring a memorable experience for all who visit.

Lake Thien Quang, nestled right before the main entrance of Thong Nhat Park (formerly known as Lenin Park), is a serene oasis in the bustling heart of Hanoi. This picturesque lake has been a beloved spot for locals to unwind and enjoy its refreshing atmosphere since time immemorial. The origins of Lake Thien Quang can be traced back to the early 19th century when it was referred to as Lien Thuy on maps from 1831. Over the years, the lake's size diminished due to urban development during the French colonial era, which saw parts of the lake filled in to make way for new streets. However, in 2003, the Hanoi Department of Transportation and Public Works undertook a significant restoration project aimed at rejuvenating the lake. This initiative involved cleaning the lakebed, reinforcing its embankments, and replacing the water to create a more natural and pristine environment. Additionally, they planted numerous trees, established small flower gardens, installed benches, and constructed clean public restrooms around the lake, enhancing the overall visitor experience.

The tranquil waters of Lake Thien Quang shimmer beautifully in the morning light, offering visitors a peaceful retreat from the city’s hustle and bustle. One of the most enchanting sights at the lake is the presence of swans, which were transferred from Hoan Kiem Lake in 2018. Initially, a flock of twelve swans made the move, but their number has since dwindled to half. These elegant birds are managed by a dedicated team and are usually kept within a fenced area for protection. On weekends, however, the swans are allowed to roam freely across the lake, creating a mesmerizing spectacle as they glide gracefully over the water. The sight of these swans swimming serenely on the calm waters often captivates visitors, providing a moment of tranquility that helps alleviate daily stresses and pressures.

For those seeking a leisurely afternoon, Lake Thien Quang offers several charming cafes nearby where one can enjoy a cup of coffee while taking in the serene views. Among the recommended spots are All Day Coffee, Cafe 61, PALM Cafe, Echoes Cafe, and Ancient Coffee. Each of these establishments provides a cozy ambiance and affordable prices, ranging from just 15,000 to 30,000 Vietnamese Dong per cup, making them ideal places to relax and savor the peaceful surroundings. Visitors can also stroll around the lake, engaging in conversations with friends or family, or simply sitting on the stone benches to soak in the tranquil beauty of the area. In addition to the lake itself, there are several notable attractions located near Lake Thien Quang. The Hoa Lo Prison, now transformed into the Hoa Lo Museum, stands as a testament to Vietnam’s tumultuous history. Formerly a notorious prison that housed many prominent revolutionaries during the anti-French and anti-American wars, it now serves as an educational site where visitors can learn about the harsh conditions endured by prisoners in the past. Another significant historical site is the Temple of Literature – Imperial Academy, founded in 1070 during the reign of King Ly Thanh Tong. This ancient institution, which once served as the first national university in Vietnam, continues to attract students and scholars who come to seek academic blessings. Nearby, the Times City Aquarium houses over 30,000 marine species in three million cubic meters of seawater, offering visitors a glimpse into the vast underwater world. Lastly, the Thong Nhat Park, known as the lungs of the city, provides a verdant escape with its year-round greenery, making it a popular destination for nature lovers.
