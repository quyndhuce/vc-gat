Ha Noi, pronounced [haː˨˩ noj˧˨ʔ] and known internationally as Hanoi, is the capital and second most populous municipality of Vietnam, boasting a population of 8,807,523 as of 2025. Spanning an area of 3,358.6 km² (1,296.8 mi²), Ha Noi is a bustling metropolis that hosted 18.7 million domestic and international visitors in 2022. The city's economy is robust, ranking second in terms of gross regional domestic product among all Vietnamese provinces and municipalities, reaching US$48 billion in 2023, just behind Ho Chi Minh City.

Ha Noi is home to numerous significant institutions, including 78 foreign embassies, the headquarters of the Vietnam People's Army (VPA), the Vietnam National University system, and various governmental organizations. The city's rich history is reflected in its landmarks such as the Imperial Citadel of Thăng Long, the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, Hoàn Kiếm Lake, West Lake, and Ba Vì National Park, which lies near the city’s outskirts. Architecturally, Ha Noi showcases a diverse range of styles, from pre-colonial Vietnamese structures to French colonial buildings, brutalist apartments, and the chaotic yet characteristic tube houses that emerged during the city's rapid expansion in the 20th century.

The origins of Ha Noi trace back to 1010 when Emperor Lý Thái Tổ of the Lý dynasty established it as the capital of the imperial nation Đại Việt, naming it Thăng Long ('ascending dragon'). In 1428, King Lê Lợi renamed the city to Đông Kinh ('eastern capital'), a title it retained until 1789. The Nguyễn dynasty relocated the national capital to Huế in 1802, and Ha Noi received its current name in 1831. During the colonial period, Ha Noi served as the capital of French Indochina from 1902 to 1945 and of the French protectorate of Tonkin from 1883 to 1949. Following the August Revolution and the dissolution of the Nguyễn dynasty, the Democratic Republic of Vietnam (DRV) declared Ha Noi the capital of the newly independent country. From 1949 to 1954, Ha Noi was part of the State of Vietnam, and then again became the capital of the DRV until 1976. Since 1976, Ha Noi has been the capital of the unified Socialist Republic of Vietnam.
