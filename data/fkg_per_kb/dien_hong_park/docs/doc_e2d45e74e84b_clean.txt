Dien Hong Park, also known as Frog Fountain Park, is located on Ngo Quyen Street in Hoan Kiem District, Hanoi. Initially built by the French, it was named Chavassieux Garden in honor of Leon Jean Laurent Chavassieux, a former Deputy Governor-General of Indochina. The park was part of a memorial project initiated in 1897, with the final design chosen from submissions by architect Harlay, and construction carried out by contractor Guillaume. In 1899, the Hanoi City Council decided to place a fountain at the center of the garden, which would serve as a focal point for public events and attract both Europeans and locals. The fountain, completed in 1901, featured a large square stone pillar surrounded by bronze frogs spouting water.

During the colonial period, the park was called Chavassieux Square. In early 1945, after Japan overthrew French rule in Indochina, Tran Van Lai was appointed as the first Vietnamese mayor of Hanoi and renamed the park Dien Hong, symbolizing national unity following independence.

Over the years, the park has faced significant deterioration. By the early 2010s, it lacked signage and its central stone pillar showed signs of cracking, posing a risk of collapse. In 2019, the Hoan Kiem District proposed a restoration project for the fountain, which was included in plans to renovate the park. Despite the deteriorating condition of the fountain, which required steel bands to prevent collapse, the park continued to host cultural events such as the opening ceremony of the Spring-Summer Fashion Week in 2020.

In 2021, issues like peeling paint, uneven ground, sparse vegetation, and outdated facilities were noted. The Hoan Kiem District responded by planning renovations to improve pathways, add greenery, and modernize amenities while preserving the historical value of the site. The district collaborated with the PRX Institute and consultancy firm DE-SO to develop a sustainable urban renewal plan that integrates contemporary design with heritage conservation. This initiative aims to maintain the park's cultural significance while enhancing its functionality for the community.

Dien Hong Park remains one of the most beautiful gardens in Hanoi, recognized for its historic fountain, which is considered the oldest in the city. It serves as a popular spot for wedding photos and leisure activities, attracting both local residents and foreign visitors who appreciate its architectural beauty.
