The construction of the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum officially began on September 2, 1973, at the site of the former grandstand in the middle of Ba Dinh Square. This was where President Ho Chi Minh stood before the entire nation to read the *Declaration of Independence*, proclaiming the birth of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam, and presided over major rallies that marked important milestones in the country's history.

The Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum is located at 25 Hung Vuong Street, Dien Bien Ward, Ba Dinh District, in the heart of Hanoi. However, visitors wishing to pay their respects to President Ho Chi Minh during a Hanoi tour must enter through the gate of the Ho Chi Minh Museum at 19 Ngoc Ha Street, Ba Dinh District, following the instructions of the Mausoleum's management board or joining the queue of visitors entering the Mausoleum.
