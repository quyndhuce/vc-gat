The Hanoi Museum, also known as Bảo tàng Hà Nội, is situated in the Nam Từ Liêm district of Hanoi, Vietnam. This institution was established to commemorate the millennium anniversary of Hanoi and officially opened its doors to the public in 2010. Covering an expansive area of nearly 54,000 square meters, the museum houses a vast collection of over 50,000 artifacts that trace the rich history, culture, heritage, and architectural evolution of both Hanoi and Vietnam. The exhibition space within the museum features an inverted pyramid structure, which adds a distinctive architectural element to the building. Through these exhibits, visitors can explore the thousand-year legacy of Hanoi and gain insight into the cultural and historical significance of the region.
