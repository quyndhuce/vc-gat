The Jingzhen Octagonal Pavilion, situated in Jingzhen Village within Mengzhe Township of Menghai County, Xishuangbanna Dai Autonomous Prefecture, Yunnan Province, stands as a testament to architectural and cultural heritage. Constructed in 1701, its primary purpose was to address a swarm of angry wasps, making it a unique structure with historical significance. The pavilion measures 15.42 meters in height and spans 8.6 meters in width, comprising three distinct sections: the base, the main body, and the roof. Over the years, the Octagonal Pavilion faced destruction multiple times but was meticulously restored in 1978. As a Dai Buddhist building, it holds religious importance and has been recognized for its cultural value by being included in the third batch of Major Historical and Cultural Sites Protected at the National Level by the Chinese State Council in 1988.
