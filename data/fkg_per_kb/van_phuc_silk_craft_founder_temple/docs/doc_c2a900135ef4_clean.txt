In the heart of Ha Dong District, nestled amidst the bustling life of a thousand-year-old weaving village, lies the Van Phuc Silk Craft Founder Temple, a serene and sacred sanctuary that embodies the essence of the imperial land. This revered temple, dedicated to the memory of Thành hoàng A Lã Thị Nương, a talented and virtuous woman, is a testament to the enduring legacy of the craft village. According to legend, A Lã Thị Nương, a skilled weaver and silkworm breeder, stopped in this very spot, imparting her knowledge to the villagers, teaching them the secrets of mulberry cultivation, silkworm rearing, and silk reeling and weaving. The temple's establishment was a tribute to her immense contributions, as well as a means to preserve the name "Van Phuc," symbolizing the aspiration for a multitude of good things and eternal prosperity.

As one approaches the temple, the imposing ancient architectural block rises up, its extremely graceful carved lines a testament to the skill of the artisans who built it. The deep red color of the mái ngói mũi hài blends seamlessly with the dark brown lim wood columns, immediately catching the eye. The unique identifying feature of the temple is the splendidly red-lacquered and gold-gilded hoành phi, majestically placed in the main hall. This sacred structure quietly nestles under the shade of a lush ancient banyan tree, adjacent to the calm, clear blue lake surface.

The temple's cultural significance is deeply rooted in the history of the craft village, which has been a hub of silk production for centuries. The villagers, who have been weaving and dyeing silk for generations, come to the temple to pay homage to their ancestors and seek blessings for their craft. During the village festival, the atmosphere becomes even more sacred and lively, with solemn palanquin processions and sacrificial rituals taking place within the temple's walls. As visitors from afar join the villagers in their reverent spiritual space, the air is filled with the sweet scent of incense and the soft hum of shuttle looms, transporting all who enter to a world of serenity and tranquility.

The Van Phuc Silk Craft Founder Temple is a testament to the enduring vitality of the craft village, a symbol of the community's dedication to preserving their heritage and traditions. As one stands before the altar, wreathed in wafting incense smoke, listening to the faint sound of shuttles echoing from the weaving workshops, one's heart is filled with a deep sense of connection to the past, and a profound appreciation for the enduring legacy of the Van Phuc Silk Craft.
