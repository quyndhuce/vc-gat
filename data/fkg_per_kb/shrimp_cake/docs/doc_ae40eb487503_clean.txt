Shrimp Cake, known as "Moon shrimp cake" in simplified Chinese as 月亮虾饼 (yuèliàng xiābǐng) and traditional Chinese as 月亮蝦餅, is a beloved Taiwanese snack often found in local Yunnanese-Thai restaurants. This delectable dish is crafted by pounding together shrimp, garlic, and pork fat, which are then spread onto a circular spring roll wrapper. A second wrapper is placed over the mixture, and the resulting cake is pan-fried until golden. It is typically served with a side of sweet chili sauce. The name "Moon shrimp cake" is believed to originate from the shape of the cake, which resembles a glowing full moon.
