Nestled along the banks of the Red River, on the outskirts of Hanoi in Gia Lâm District, lies the historic village of Bat Trang, renowned for its exquisite pottery and porcelain. Founded during the Lý Dynasty, when the imperial court relocated to Thăng Long, skilled artisans from the Bồ Bát region arrived at this fertile alluvial plain to establish their craft. The village's name, "Bát Tràng," translates to "the great courtyard," symbolizing the grandeur and dedication to pottery that has defined the community since its inception.

Bat Trang Pottery Village is believed to have been established as early as the 14th or 15th century, though some historians suggest it may be even older. Its strategic location, rich in clay deposits and situated between the ancient trading hubs of Thăng Long and Phố Hiến, facilitated the export of its ceramics both domestically and internationally. The village's proximity to these historical centers ensured that its products reached a wide audience, contributing significantly to its cultural and economic importance.

The architectural landscape of Bat Trang is characterized by towering structures that resemble giant pottery wheels, with undulating spirals rising towards the sky. These buildings, constructed with rustic terracotta bricks, feature rough surfaces and intricate spiral cone blocks that give them a distinctive appearance. They stand majestically beside tranquil canals, adjacent to busy roads teeming with vehicles transporting ceramics to various destinations.

Cultural activities in Bat Trang are deeply intertwined with its pottery traditions. Visitors can experience hands-on pottery making, learning the techniques that have been passed down through generations. During major festivals, the village comes alive with the sacred water procession, where villagers carry water from the main river to the communal hall, or đình, in a ritual that celebrates the village's heritage and unity. The process of transforming raw clay into intricate ceramic pieces is a testament to the skill and dedication of the artisans, who work tirelessly day and night at their pottery wheels.

Each piece of pottery produced in Bat Trang carries a unique story, reflecting the village's rich history and the passion of its people. From the cool, indigo-glazed vases to intricately designed porcelain, every item embodies the essence of Bat Trang's legacy. The village not only serves as a vibrant open-air museum but also as a living testament to the enduring spirit of craftsmanship and tradition.
