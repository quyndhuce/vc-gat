The Thang Long Water Puppetry Theatre stands as a unique cultural landmark in Vietnam, renowned for its continuous performances of water puppetry throughout the year. Established over five decades ago on October 10, 1969, the theatre has become a significant destination for both domestic and international tourists, offering a glimpse into the rich cultural heritage of Vietnam through its vibrant and intricate performances. The theatre's dedication to preserving and promoting traditional Vietnamese art forms is evident in its extensive repertoire, which includes a variety of shows that cater to diverse audiences, from children to adults.

One of the most celebrated performances at the Thang Long Water Puppetry Theatre is "Hoàng Đế Cờ Lau," which tells the story of the first emperor of Đại Cồ Việt, Đinh Tiên Hoàng, also known as Đinh Bộ Lĩnh. This play showcases the early life of Đinh Bộ Lĩnh, highlighting his intelligence and extraordinary abilities even as a young boy tending cattle. The performance not only entertains but also educates visitors about the historical roots of Vietnam, blending folklore with factual history in a captivating manner.

Another notable production is "Mơ Rồng," directed by Lê Quý Dương, which pushes the boundaries of traditional water puppetry. This innovative show breaks many conventions of the genre, presenting a more experimental approach to the art form. It challenges the traditional norms while still maintaining the essence of water puppetry, making it a must-see for those interested in the evolution of this ancient art.

The theatre also stages "Hồn Trương Ba Da Hàng Thịt," a classic theatrical work adapted from the original play by Lưu Quang Vũ. This adaptation brings together elements of traditional Vietnamese culture and modern storytelling techniques, creating a unique experience that resonates with audiences across different age groups. The play has been performed in various formats, including drama, opera, and traditional theater, but its rendition as a water puppet show adds a new dimension to the narrative, showcasing the versatility of the art form.
