Ho Chi Minh City features a diverse range of architectural styles, including French colonial buildings in District 1, characterized by grand facades with intricate ironwork and pastel-colored exteriors. Historic Chinese-Vietnamese assembly halls and temples are found in Cholon (District 5), often featuring red and gold decorations and traditional Chinese motifs. Modern landmarks such as the Independence Palace, Bitexco Financial Tower, and Landmark 81 Tower stand out with sleek, contemporary designs. The city's streets, particularly Phạm Ngũ Lão and Bùi Viện, are lined with vibrant markets and shops. Surrounding the city are numerous rivers and canals, with the Saigon River being the largest waterway.
