Truc Bach Lake, a serene gem nestled in the heart of Hanoi, is a tranquil oasis amidst the bustling city. Located in Ba Dinh District, this oval-shaped lake is a masterpiece of natural beauty, with its slender and pretty size unfolding before one's eyes. The deep blue hue of the calm water, reflecting the sky and clouds, is a sight to behold. The lake's unique identifying feature is the pure white swan pedal boats gently floating and parting the waves, creating a picturesque scene that is quintessentially Hanoi.

As one approaches the lake, the surrounding streets, lined with shops and eateries, come alive with the sounds of laughter and chatter. The gentle rustling of the weeping willows, swaying in the breeze, adds to the serene ambiance. The lake's calm waters are a popular spot for walking, relaxing, and light exercise, making it an ideal destination for both locals and tourists.

The lake's rich history dates back to the 17th century, when it was part of the vast waters of Ho Tay Lake. The Cổ Ngư dike, built during this period, separated the lake from its larger counterpart, giving rise to the name Truc Bach, which originates from the remnants of an ancient palace of Chúa Trịnh. The palace, where court ladies were once held under house arrest, was famous for its production of exquisite white silk, known as "lụa làng Trúc" or Trúc village silk. The beautiful silk, woven day and night, became a symbol of the lake's cultural significance, and the name Truc Bach has been passed down through the ages.

Today, Truc Bach Lake is not only a historical landmark but also a cultural symbol of Hanoi's urban landscape. It is a rare peaceful haven, where visitors can anchor their purest emotions amidst the bustling pace of city life. Whether one chooses to leisurely paddle a swan boat, sip coffee in the breeze, or enjoy the renowned phở cuốn, Truc Bach Lake is a must-visit destination for anyone seeking a tranquil escape from the city's chaos.
