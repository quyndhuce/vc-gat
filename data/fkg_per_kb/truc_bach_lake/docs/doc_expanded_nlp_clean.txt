Truc Bach Lake (Hồ Trúc Bạch) is located in Ba Dinh district, Hanoi. It is a historic urban lake.
Truc Bach Lake is historically connected to the larger West Lake landscape and to craft and neighborhood histories.
Thanh Nien Road separates Truc Bach Lake from West Lake.
The Ngu Xa peninsula borders part of Truc Bach Lake.
Characteristic physical features include calm water, tree-lined embankments, lakeside buildings, cafes and small religious sites.
Truc Bach Lake is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Lake shore, Thanh Nien Road, Ngu Xa peninsula.
The site is associated with local food traditions, neighborhood heritage and lakeside recreation.
Truc Bach Lake is located immediately east of West Lake.
The lake is bordered by residential streets and restaurants.
Truc Bach is associated with the history of local silk production.
Ngu Xa lies in the wider Truc Bach area.
Truc Bach Lake is associated with the capture of American pilot John McCain in 1967.
A memorial near the lake records the wartime incident.
The lake surface is surrounded by urban development.
The shoreline includes pedestrian access and planted sections.
Truc Bach Lake is smaller than West Lake.
The two lakes are geographically distinct bodies of water.
