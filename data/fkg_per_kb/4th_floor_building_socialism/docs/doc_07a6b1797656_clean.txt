As visitors ascend to the 4th floor of the Hanoi Museum, they are transported to a bygone era, one that whispers tales of resilience and community spirit. The "Building Socialism" exhibition, a nostalgic slice of modern history, unfolds like a tapestry of memories, weaving together the threads of Hanoi's journey through the tumultuous years following the war. This space recreates a period when the capital city was not only the political hub of the country but also a beacon of hope, where the aspirations for construction, development, and stability shone bright amidst the tumultuous backdrop of the 20th century.

The exhibition's chronological arrangement of documentary photographs, propaganda posters, and daily life artifacts transports viewers to a time when factories hummed day and night, labor and production movements spread far and wide, and the collective spirit became an integral part of the lives of Hanoi's residents. The faded colors of red, pale yellow, and brown evoke the feeling of a bygone era, as propaganda posters, ration coupons, radios, bicycles, and everyday items from the subsidy period come alive.

The setting is a veritable time capsule, with models of collective housing, images of factories and construction sites, and historical information boards serving as a testament to the city's transformation. The stories of sharing, thriftiness, and the will to overcome difficulties often pause elderly visitors, as if they are revisiting a part of their own memories. For younger visitors, this space offers a glimpse into a Hanoi that is vastly different from the present – simple, lacking, yet rich in community spirit.

As visitors leave the exhibition, they carry with them a deeper understanding of the generations of people who contributed to shaping the face of Hanoi today, from the most resilient and simple things. The "Building Socialism" exhibition is a poignant reminder of the city's capacity to rise from the ashes, a testament to the indomitable spirit of its people, and a celebration of the collective efforts that have made Hanoi the vibrant, thriving metropolis it is today.
