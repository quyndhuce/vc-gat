As travelers step through the grand main gate of Văn Miếu Môn, they are immediately enveloped in the serene atmosphere of the Nhập Đạo section, the introductory space of the revered Văn Miếu Quốc Tử Giám complex. This sacred area, shaped since the nascent period of national higher education during the Lý dynasty, bears a name imbued with a profoundly deep philosophy of life. The name "Nhập Đạo" itself is a testament to the profound significance of this space, as it represents the first step towards shedding the mundane and embarking on the path of moral cultivation, self-improvement, and character development for the nho sinh of yesteryear.

As one strolls through the vast rectangular courtyard, the deep red hue of moss-covered bricks blends seamlessly with the lush green lawn, immediately catching the eye. The unique identifying feature of this space is the majestic main path, shaded by two rows of ancient trees, which seems to whisper secrets of the past. The tranquil gạch vồ wall, adjacent to the gate that shields all the city's noise, envelops this green space, creating an oasis of serenity amidst the bustling capital.

The Nhập Đạo section is a masterpiece of traditional Vietnamese court architecture, with its intricate stone carvings, ornate lanterns, and delicate ceramic tiles. The air is filled with the sweet scent of blooming flowers and the soft chirping of birds, creating a sense of peace and tranquility that is hard to find in the midst of the city's hustle and bustle.

As a UNESCO heritage site and a national special relic, the Nhập Đạo section is a must-visit destination for anyone interested in Vietnamese history, culture, and architecture. Visitors can stroll leisurely along the moss-covered brick paths, taking in the serene atmosphere and admiring the intricate details of the ancient structures. On special occasions like the start of the school year or before major exams, students return to this sacred space, clasping their hands in prayer, seeking peace of mind and clear wisdom.

As the sun begins to set, the Nhập Đạo section transforms into a romantic haven, with the soft glow of lanterns casting a warm, golden light over the courtyard. It is here that one can truly experience the essence of Vietnamese culture, where the past and present blend seamlessly together, and the soul is cleansed of its worries and doubts.
