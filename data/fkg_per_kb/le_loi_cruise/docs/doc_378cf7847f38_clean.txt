As we step into the revered Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre, situated on the picturesque Dinh Tien Hoang Street, we are transported to a realm of artistic splendor, nestled beside the ancient beauty of Hoan Kiem Lake. This esteemed institution, which has proudly borne the name Thang Long since 1969, is a sanctuary of cultural heritage, its roots tracing back to the era when King Ly Thai To moved the capital. The theatre's name is a testament to the majestic pride of the nation, as the legend of King Le Thai To boarding a dragon boat to return the precious sword to the Golden Turtle God is reenacted on stage.

As we take our seats, the air is electric with anticipation, the scent of incense wafting through the auditorium. The lights dim, and the performance begins, with the majestic form of the dragon boat emerging onto the stage. The brilliant vermilion and bright yellow colors of the lacquer layer on the wooden core of the boat catch the eye, as the imposing king and his tiny army of soldiers come into view. The moment the giant Golden Turtle emerges to take the gleaming sword in its mouth is a breathtaking spectacle, set against the gently rippling lake surface, nestled right in front of a rustic, hanging bamboo screen.

As the performance unfolds, the entire auditorium is filled with a heroic atmosphere, the urgent festive drumbeats blending with the resonant, melodious horns. International tourists and local residents of the capital alike are transfixed, their eyes glued to the stage as the dragon boat glides gently across the shimmering water. The glowing sword is returned to the Golden Turtle God, and the water gradually calms, leaving a lasting impression on the audience. This performance not only showcases the pinnacle of puppetry control artistry but also delivers an experience that touches the very depths of historical pride amidst the rhythm of contemporary urban life.

As we leave the theatre, the legend of King Le Thai To's return of the precious sword to the Golden Turtle God lingers in our minds, a poignant reminder of the nation's age-old aspiration for peace. The Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre is a testament to the enduring power of cultural heritage, a symbol of the nation's rich history and cultural identity.
