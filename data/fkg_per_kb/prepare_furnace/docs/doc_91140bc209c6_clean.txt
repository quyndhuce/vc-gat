Nestled peacefully on the tree-lined peninsula shore by the windy West Lake, the Prepare Furnace at the Ngũ Xã bronze casting workshop has been a revered ritualistic space for centuries. This ancient furnace, built from clay mixed with rice husks, stands as a testament to the ingenuity and craftsmanship of generations of artisans. The rustic grayish-brown hue, blended with the black of charcoal, immediately catches the eye, as if the very essence of the capital's culture has been infused into its structure.

The Ngũ Xã workshop, founded nearly five centuries ago, was born from the gathering of elite artisans from five renowned bronze casting villages, who lit the first sacred fires to offer to kings and lords. This sacred ritual, imbued with the spirit of the land, has been passed down through the ages, with each generation of artisans meticulously preserving the essence of Thăng Long.

As one approaches the furnace, the air is filled with the pungent smell of smoke, a sensory cue that signals the beginning of the casting process. The copper molds, smoke-stained brick wall, and shaded trees outside the small alley by the lake create a unique visual tapestry, a testament to the workshop's rich history and cultural significance.

The towering cylindrical kiln block, with its deep kiln door glowing with brilliant red-orange sparks, stands as a beacon of creativity and innovation. This primitive structure, nestled amidst the clutter of copper molds and a smoke-stained brick wall, is a testament to the ingenuity and craftsmanship of the artisans who have shaped the very fabric of Vietnamese culture.

As visitors approach the furnace, they are struck by the sense of reverence and awe that permeates the air. The furnace, a symbol of the city's cultural heritage, is a reminder of the countless hours of labor and dedication that have gone into preserving the art of bronze casting. Standing before this magnificent structure, one cannot help but feel a deep admiration for the human spirit, which has been distilled into the very essence of the furnace.

In this sacred space, the boundaries between past and present, tradition and innovation, are blurred. The Prepare Furnace at Ngũ Xã is a living embodiment of the city's cultural identity, a testament to the enduring power of human creativity and ingenuity.
