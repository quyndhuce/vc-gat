Huu Tiep Lake (Hồ Hữu Tiệp) is located in Ngoc Ha neighborhood, Ba Dinh district, Hanoi. It is a war-history memorial lake.
The lake is associated with wreckage of a US B-52 bomber from the December 1972 air campaign over Hanoi.
Aircraft fragments lie within the lake basin.
Houses and local streets enclose the compact water body.
Characteristic physical features include small neighborhood lake, visible aircraft wreckage, surrounding houses and narrow lanes.
