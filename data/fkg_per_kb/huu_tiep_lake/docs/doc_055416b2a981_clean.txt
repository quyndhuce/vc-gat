Huu Tiep Lake, also known as B-52 Lake, is a small artificial freshwater lake situated in the ward of Ngoc Ha, Hanoi. Originally part of a larger river, most of the waterway was filled in 1994 to create residential areas, leaving Huu Tiep Lake and another unnamed lake nearby, separated by 55 Hoang Hoa Tham lane.

This lake holds significant historical importance due to its connection with a tragic event from the Vietnam War. On December 12, 1972, during Operation Linebacker II, a B-52 Stratofortress was shot down by the 285th regiment of the 363rd division in the Vietnam Air Defence - Air Force Service. Parts of the wreckage from this aircraft now lie at the bottom of Huu Tiep Lake, giving it its alternative name. In recognition of its historical significance, the lake and the remnants of the B-52 were designated as a national historical vestige on April 22, 1992.

However, over time, the condition of the lake and the ruins deteriorated, prompting concern among locals and visitors. In response to these issues, the People's Council of Hanoi initiated a renovation project in April 2021. The restoration work was completed in time for the 50th anniversary of Operation Linebacker II in 2022, allowing tourists to visit and learn about this poignant piece of history.
