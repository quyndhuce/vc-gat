Nestled in the heart of Hanoi's Hoàn Kiếm District, the Ly Thai To statue stands as a monumental tribute to one of Vietnam's most revered kings, Lý Thái Tổ. Erected in 2004 in preparation for the millennium anniversary of Thăng Long, the statue celebrates the enlightened monarch who played a pivotal role in the country's history. Located near the picturesque Hoàn Kiếm Lake, this bronze masterpiece is crafted from pure copper, its surface developing a distinctive dark blue-green patina over time, adding to its regal appearance.

The statue depicts King Lý Thái Tổ standing gallantly, his right hand firmly grasping the scroll of the historic Chiếu dời đô edict, which he issued in 1010. According to legend, upon arriving at the site of what would become Hanoi, Lý Thái Tổ witnessed a golden dragon ascending from the Red River, prompting him to rename the city Đại La to Thăng Long, marking the beginning of a new era of prosperity and glory for the nation. This significant event is immortalized in the statue, serving as a powerful reminder of the king's visionary leadership.

Surrounded by a spacious stone-paved square, the statue is enveloped by cool green canopies that provide shade and a serene atmosphere. The area around the statue is a vibrant cultural hub, attracting locals and tourists alike. Early mornings and late afternoons see the square bustling with activity as people gather to exercise, roller skate, or simply stroll and enjoy the tranquil ambiance. On weekends and during Tết holidays, the space transforms into a lively venue for music stages and street festivals, blending traditional festivities with contemporary entertainment.

The Ly Thai To statue is more than just a historical landmark; it is a symbol of Hanoi's enduring spirit and cultural heritage. Its presence serves as a poignant reminder of the city's rich past while seamlessly integrating into the dynamic fabric of modern life. Visitors are often moved by the heroic vitality that seems to emanate from the statue, creating a profound connection between the millennium-old legacy and the vibrant energy of today's Hanoi.
