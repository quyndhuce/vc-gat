On the evening of August 24th, the Trung tâm hội nghị tỉnh Bình Định hosted a special artistic program titled "Ký ức quê hương" with the theme "Giai điệu ân tình - quê hương Bình Định." This event was organized by the People's Committee of Binh Dinh Province in collaboration with the Vietravel Group, the Association of Binh Dinh Compatriots in Ho Chi Minh City, the Nutifood Corporation, and the creative company Mãnh Hổ. The purpose of this night was to honor and express gratitude towards musicians and vocal performers from Binh Dinh who have left significant marks in the Vietnamese music scene.

The event not only celebrated the contributions of these artists but also underscored commitments to environmental protection and sustainable development. It was free for attendees and live-streamed online, making it accessible to people across the country. Musicians and vocal performers from Binh Dinh, such as Phạm Thế Mỹ, Giao Tiên, Trần Long Ẩn, Hàn Châu, Tô Quốc Cơ, Ngô Tín, and Vũ Thanh, among others, performed songs they had composed, which resonated deeply with the audience. These compositions were interwoven with popular contemporary tunes favored by young people and the general public on social media platforms.

The evening featured a blend of musical performances and fashion shows, showcasing the creativity and cultural richness of Binh Dinh. Two renowned designers, Hoàng Tony and Hà Thanh Việt, presented their collections inspired by the colors and life of their hometown. Their designs went beyond mere clothing, serving as messages about the culture and people of Binh Dinh. Models including supermodel Anh Thư, Mr World Vietnam 2024 finalist Võ Minh Toại, Miss Beauty Nguyễn Thảo Vy, Mạc Huyền Trân, and nearly twenty models from Binh Dinh participated in the runway show.

During the event, the People's Committee of Binh Dinh Province and the Vietravel Group announced ambassadors for the "Go Green" campaign, aimed at promoting responsible tourism that is environmentally friendly. They emphasized the importance of concrete actions in developing tourism and festivals in Binh Dinh, advocating for zero waste policies. This initiative aligns with broader efforts to preserve the natural beauty and cultural heritage of the region while fostering sustainable growth.

The atmosphere at the Trung tâm hội nghị was vibrant and filled with nostalgia, as the performances evoked memories of childhood and the unique charm of "the land of martial arts." The event highlighted the deep connection between music, fashion, and the cultural identity of Binh Dinh, offering visitors a rich tapestry of local traditions and modern expressions. Through the lens of music and fashion, the evening celebrated the enduring legacy of musicians and vocal performers from Binh Dinh, reinforcing their impact on the cultural landscape of Vietnam.
