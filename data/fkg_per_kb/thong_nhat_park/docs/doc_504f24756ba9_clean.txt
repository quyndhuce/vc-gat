Thong Nhat park is one of the largest parks in Hanoi, Vietnam. Within the park lies Lake Bảy Mẫu, located at its center. The park borders four streets: Tran Nhan Tong, Nguyen Dinh Chieu, Le Duan, and Dai Co Viet, situated within Hai Ba Trung district of Hanoi city. Despite being adjacent to these four bustling streets, Thong Nhat park remains tranquil and cool, making it an ideal choice for relaxation, recreation, group activities, or studying. There are two main entrances to the park, one on Le Duan Street near the Central Circus Theatre, and the other on Dai Co Viet Street opposite Hanoi University of Science and Technology. Visitors can easily reach the park by various means of transportation including motorcycles, buses, or cars. For those traveling by personal vehicle, there are parking spaces available outside the park. Alternatively, several bus routes such as 03A, 08B, 09B, 30, 32, 35A, 40, 41, 44, 51, 52, 144, and E03 pass through the park.

Historically, in 1954 when the country was divided between North and South Vietnam, the northern region was in the process of building socialism while the southern region was still enduring war. At that time, Southern cadres and civil servants chose a public place to gather and organize monthly meetings. This place was Thong Nhat park. The construction of Thong Nhat park was based on voluntary labor by the people of Hanoi, expressing their hope for national reunification.

For a period from 1980 to 2003, Thong Nhat park was known as Lenin Park. After the renaming of the Chi Lang flower garden as Lenin Park, Thong Nhat park reverted to its original name.
