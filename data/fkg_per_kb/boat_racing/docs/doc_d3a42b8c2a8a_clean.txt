The UIM E1 Series, an all-electric international offshore powerboat racing series sanctioned by the Union Internationale Motonautique, made its debut in February 2024 with the E1 Jeddah GP in Saudi Arabia. This series was conceived in early 2020 by Rodi Basso, a former Formula One engineer and McLaren Applied business director, and Alejandro Agag, the founder of Formula E and Extreme E. They approached Sophi Horne, who had previously focused on electric leisure boats, to design the race boat. The series was officially announced at the Monaco Yacht Club on September 25, 2020, and the boat was unveiled in June 2021, along with a partnership with Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund.

Testing of the RaceBird, the all-electric hydrofoil boat, commenced on April 21, 2022, when Luca Ferrari, a former powerboat champion, piloted the prototype on the River Po in Italy. The boat then made its public debut at the Venice Boat Show in June 2022, where model Cara Delevingne assisted with the launch. The first public race occurred in September 2022 during the World Port Days in Rotterdam, with Sam Coleman piloting the RaceBird. In September 2023, the series returned to Rotterdam, this time featuring four RaceBirds in competition, with Oban Duncan emerging as the champion.

The inaugural 2024 season featured 9 teams and 18 pilots, with Team Brady winning the championship. The second season in 2025 began in January in Jeddah, with the debut of a new team led by LeBron James. Despite the addition of new talent, Team Brady retained its championship title after seven races.

Each race weekend consists of free practice and qualifying sessions on the first day, followed by group races, a race-off, a place race, and finals on the second day. Pilots alternate driving duties throughout these sessions. Each race lasts six laps, including a mandatory short lap and long lap that can be taken at any point during the race. The course boundaries are marked by electronic anchorless buoys called SmartMarks, which are less invasive than traditional buoys.

The Blue Impact championship, introduced during the inaugural season, challenges teams to work towards a better future through activism such as aquatic conservation and restoration programs and marine habitat research. Fans and an expert jury vote for the champion at the end of the season. Team Brady won the inaugural Blue Impact Championship.

The RaceBird, designed by SeaBird Technologies and manufactured by Victory Marine, features a 150kW electric motor and a 35kWh battery capacity, allowing it to reach a top speed of 50 knots (58 MPH/93 KPH). The boat is 7.5 meters long and uses carbon fiber hydrofoils that lift the vessel when it reaches 17 knots. The cockpit is designed for a single pilot, equipped with a steering wheel and pedals similar to those found in a single-seater race car. The electric powertrain is developed by Mercury Racing, and the rechargeable batteries come from Kreisel Electric. Currently, all teams run identical boats, but the goal is to allow teams to design their own vehicles in future years.

The series initially aimed to represent various cities and countries, starting with the Venice Racing Team. However, it evolved to feature celebrity owners such as Tom Brady, Will Smith, Virat Kohli, and Rafael Nadal.
