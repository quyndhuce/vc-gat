As visitors ascend the breezy staircase axis of the Vietnamese Women's Museum on Lý Thường Kiệt Street, they are invited to step into the 3rd Floor space, themed "Women and History." This sacred chronicle unfolds, sketching the tragic yet proud portrait of women throughout the nation's history. Born from the passion to preserve heritage, this space marks the pulse of history, from the glorious legends of Bà Trưng and Bà Triệu, who rode elephants to fight invaders, to the fiery years of bombs and bullets when mothers and sisters sacrificed their youth for independence.

The angular space reveals its grandeur with deep gray tones and majestic betel-nut red, evoking a sense of reverence and solemnity. Under the warm yellow lights, transparent display cases protecting wartime memorabilia immediately catch the eye, showcasing the bravery and resilience of the female soldiers who fought for their country. The unmistakable exclusive highlight is the wall completely covered with black and white portraits of these female warriors, their faces etched with determination and courage.

As visitors wander through this space, they are transported to a bygone era, where the sounds of gunfire and explosions still echo through the corridors. The long, silent shadows along the corridor seem to whisper stories of sacrifice and devotion, as the worn-out uniforms of female guerrillas hang like ghosts, a poignant reminder of the human cost of war. Amidst the bustling pace of life, this place maintains an atmosphere of nostalgia and affection, as visitors often linger for a long time to quietly read faded love letters or gaze at the worn-out uniforms of female guerrillas.

Touching that realm of memory, our hearts are suddenly filled with an indescribable emotion, overflowing with boundless admiration and gratitude for the heroic, indomitable, loyal, and resourceful spirit of these "steel roses" who steadfastly shaped the form of the nation. As we stand before the portraits of these women, we are reminded of the power of courage, resilience, and determination, and the profound impact they had on the course of Vietnamese history.
