Bay Mau Lake is a freshwater lake located within Thong Nhat Park in Hanoi. The lake covers an area of approximately 28 hectares and features two islands: Thong Nhat Island and Hoa Binh Island.

The lake is situated slightly south of the city center, within Hai Ba Trung ward. To the south, it borders Dai Co Viet Street, while to the southeast and east, it is adjacent to Van Ho III Street, which leads to Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street. To the north, the lake is bordered by Thong Nhat Park, and to the west, it is separated by Le Duan Street. Across Le Duan Street lies another lake called Ba Mau Lake. According to ancient maps from the Hong Duc period, the southern end of Bay Mau Lake connects to the Kim Nguu River via a canal known as the Lam Khang Canal, which is now referred to as the Nam Khang Canal.

Thong Nhat Island, one of the two islands in the lake, is a flower garden connected to the park's entrance on Le Duan Street by a bridge. Hoa Binh Island, located near the eastern shore, is a tranquil spot for visitors seeking rest and relaxation. Access to this island requires the use of a boat.
