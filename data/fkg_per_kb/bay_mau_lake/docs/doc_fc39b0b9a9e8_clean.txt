Bay Mau Lake, nestled within the expansive grounds of Thong Nhat Park in Hai Ba Trung district of Hanoi, stands out as a verdant oasis amidst the bustling urban landscape. This freshwater lake, known for its significant role in the city's environmental balance, is a cherished landmark that draws both locals and tourists alike. Situated in the southern part of central Hanoi, the lake borders major thoroughfares such as Dai Co Viet to the south, Van Ho III and Nguyen Dinh Chieu to the east and southeast, and Le Duan to the west, while also sharing its northern boundary with Thong Nhat Park itself. Across from the lake to the west lies a smaller but equally popular body of water, Ba Mau Lake.

Covering approximately 28.2 hectares, Bay Mau Lake serves as a vital green lung for the city, offering a serene retreat from the hustle and bustle of daily life. The lake’s tranquil waters are complemented by two charming islands, Thong Nhat Island and Hoa Binh Island, which add to its picturesque allure. Thong Nhat Island, connected to the park via a quaint bridge, houses a vibrant flower garden, while Hoa Binh Island provides a peaceful haven for those seeking tranquility and solitude.

The name "Bay Mau Lake" derives from its original size of roughly 7 mu of land (equivalent to around 25,200 square meters), reflecting its historical significance. Over time, particularly following the establishment of Thong Nhat Park in 1960, the lake underwent extensive renovations and expansions, significantly increasing its area and becoming an integral part of the park’s lush ecosystem.

Bay Mau Lake plays a crucial role in the lives of Hanoi residents, not only due to its historical importance but also because of its environmental benefits. It acts as a natural air conditioner, helping to regulate the temperature and provide relief during the scorching summer months. The combination of the surrounding greenery and the expansive water surface creates a refreshing atmosphere, making it a perfect spot for relaxation and recreation.

Visitors can enjoy leisurely walks or bike rides along the well-paved paths that encircle the lake, flanked by tall trees providing ample shade. Early mornings and evenings are especially popular times for these activities, as people take advantage of the cooler temperatures and serene ambiance. The lake’s tranquil setting offers a peaceful escape from the city’s noise and congestion, allowing visitors to immerse themselves in nature and unwind.

For those interested in photography, Bay Mau Lake presents numerous opportunities to capture stunning images. The lush greenery, calm waters reflecting the clear sky, and the small bridges leading to the islands create ideal backdrops for memorable photos. During spring and autumn, the scenery becomes even more enchanting, attracting many to document their visits through beautiful snapshots.

In addition to walking and cycling, the lake also hosts various recreational activities. Yoga and meditation sessions are held in the cool, quiet surroundings, while sports enthusiasts can play badminton or shuttlecock in the open spaces near the lake. Fishing is another popular pastime, especially in the evenings when elderly residents gather to fish along the rocky shores.

One of the unique experiences at Bay Mau Lake is the opportunity to pedal a swan-shaped boat across the vast expanse of water. This activity allows visitors to enjoy a leisurely cruise, feeling the gentle breeze against their skin while taking in the panoramic views of the lake. It is particularly favored by couples looking for romantic outings on weekends.

The centerpiece of the lake is Thong Nhat Island, home to a historic monument dedicated to Uncle Ho and Uncle Ton, commemorating a significant event in Vietnamese history when Uncle Ton was elected as Vice President of the country. This site not only holds cultural and historical value but also serves as a venue for community events, including art performances and cultural club activities, fostering a lively atmosphere on the island.

Adjacent to Thong Nhat Island is Hoa Binh Island, accessible by small boats, which adds to the adventure of visiting the lake. The island is often used for outdoor activities and team-building exercises, providing a spacious and airy setting for gatherings and social events.

Nearby, Tran Nhan Tong pedestrian street offers additional entertainment options, especially on weekends, with street performances, folk games, flash mobs, and street food stalls, enhancing the overall experience for visitors to Bay Mau Lake.

Bay Mau Lake thus stands as a testament to the harmonious coexistence of nature and urban development, offering a blend of historical significance, recreational activities, and cultural enrichment. Its serene beauty and diverse attractions make it a must-visit destination for anyone exploring the vibrant city of Hanoi.
