Nestled within the Trống Đồng building of the Vietnam Museum of Ethnology, the exhibition area dedicated to ethnic musical instruments stands as a testament to the rich cultural heritage of Vietnam’s fifty-four ethnic groups. Since the museum’s inception, this collection has been meticulously assembled from various regions across the country, serving as a living archive that preserves the soulful essence of each community. According to ancient legends, the sounds produced by these instruments were not merely for entertainment; they held sacred significance, bridging the gap between humans and deities, ensuring bountiful harvests, and transforming simple objects into powerful symbols of indigenous beliefs.

The exhibition space is a vibrant tapestry of auditory experiences, where visitors can immerse themselves in the resonant tones of the cồng chiêng, a grand set of bronze drums that reverberate through the halls via a sophisticated sound system. These majestic instruments, often played during festive occasions, evoke a sense of communal celebration and spiritual connection. Additionally, the delicate sound of the đàn t'rưng, a bamboo zither, gently trickles through the air, offering a serene contrast to the more robust beats of the cồng chiêng.

The visual spectacle of the exhibit is equally captivating. Long glass display cases stretch out, housing a diverse array of musical instruments, each one a unique piece of cultural artistry. The rich golden hues of bamboo and the subdued patina of bronze create a striking visual palette that draws the eye. Among these treasures, the grand cồng chiêng set suspended on a rustic wooden stand stands out as a centerpiece, its presence commanding respect and admiration. Under the warm glow of yellow lights, these artifacts rest quietly, their silent forms speaking volumes about the traditions and beliefs they represent.

This space serves as a vital cultural hub, not only for tourists but also for scholars and enthusiasts seeking to understand the profound cultural significance of these instruments. Each artifact tells a story of resilience, creativity, and the enduring spirit of the Vietnamese people. Through the rhythmic beats and melodic tunes, visitors are transported back in time, gaining a deeper appreciation for the intense desire for life and harmony that has characterized the Vietnamese ancestors since ancient times.
