In the heart of Hanoi, nestled within the front courtyard of the Hanoi Museum on Pham Hung Street, lies the Dong Buom Pavilion, a captivating architectural wonder that seamlessly blends art, culture, and indigenous memories. This creative space, introduced in 2025 as part of the Top 10 Awards, is a testament to the city's commitment to preserving its rich cultural heritage.

As one approaches the pavilion, the first impression is of three towering structures, resembling giant bamboo sails, unfurled as if catching the wind in the spacious courtyard. The golden-brown color of the bamboo stands out under the sun, with the woven slats creating a sense of both sturdiness and softness. The inverted pyramid architecture of the museum, interspersed with green trees and a spacious plaza, provides a striking backdrop to the bamboo structures.

The name "Dong Buom" evokes the image of setting sail, a highly symbolic expression of movement, exploration, and faith in the future. The rustic bamboo material, however, keeps this journey grounded, as if every step forward carries the shadow of Vietnamese tradition. This dichotomy between modernity and heritage is a recurring theme throughout the pavilion, as visitors are encouraged to explore the displayed architectural projects and immerse themselves in the cultural significance of the space.

As the sun casts its warm rays upon the bamboo structures, the pavilion transforms into a vibrant hub of activity, with residents and tourists alike strolling leisurely around the exhibits, taking photos, and simply standing under the shade of the "sails" to feel the harmony between art and public space. The sense of lightness and freedom that pervades the atmosphere is palpable, as if the pavilion is reminding visitors that even in a modern city, the simplest materials and deeply rooted memories can still evoke a sense of connection to the past.

The Dong Buom Pavilion is a testament to the city's commitment to preserving its cultural heritage, and its unique blend of modernity and tradition makes it a must-visit destination for anyone interested in exploring the rich cultural landscape of Hanoi.
