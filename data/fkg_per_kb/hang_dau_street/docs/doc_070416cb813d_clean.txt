Hang Dau Street is a street that stretches over 270 meters from Tran Nhut Duat Street to Hang Than Street. This street was once part of the old National Route 1, leading directly to the Long Bien Bridge.

Located in the Nguyen Trung Truc ward of Ba Dinh district and Dong Xuan ward of Hoan Kiem district, Hang Dau Street runs parallel to and intersects with several other streets including Tran Nhut Duat, Nguyen Thep, Hong Phuc, Hang Giay, and Hang Than.

Historically, Hang Dau Street was named for its former trade in various types of beans. At one time, this area belonged to the villages of Phuc Lam in the Tả Túc district and Nghia Lap in the Hoi Tuc district of the old Tho Xuan county. Today, it falls within the Dong Xuan ward of Hoan Kiem district. During the French colonial period, the street retained its name as Hang Dau (rue des Graines), and the section from Phung Hung Street to the road leading up to the Long Bien Bridge was part of the old National Route 1.

Several bus routes run through Hang Dau Street, including routes 01, 14, 18, 22, 23, and 36, which terminate at the end of the street.
