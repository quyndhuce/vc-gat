Nestled in the heart of Hanoi, the Vietnam Museum of Ethnology stands as a vibrant hub of cultural activity, its sprawling grounds serving as a living museum that brings to life the rich tapestry of Vietnamese traditions. The museum’s vast, flat rammed earth courtyard, painted in the rustic golden hue of bamboo poles, is a testament to the enduring legacy of the thousand-year-old wet-rice civilization. Here, visitors are invited to step into a world where ancient games like stilt walking, bamboo dancing, and ném còn (a game involving throwing rings around a stick) are not just forms of entertainment but deeply rooted cultural practices imbued with historical significance.

Originating from the fertile plains of Vietnam, these traditional activities have been passed down through generations, each game carrying within it tales of ancient fertility rituals and communal wishes for abundant harvests. The rhythmic clacking of bamboo poles during bamboo dancing, for instance, echoes the sounds of a bygone era when such dances were performed to invoke the spirits for favorable weather conditions. Similarly, the spirited tug-of-war matches held in the courtyard reflect the unity and strength of the community, embodying the collective spirit of the fifty-four ethnic groups that make up Vietnam’s diverse population.

The courtyard itself is a bustling space, its flat surface ideal for the dynamic movements required by these traditional games. Rows of criss-crossed bamboo poles rhythmically opening and closing create a mesmerizing visual spectacle, drawing the attention of both locals and tourists alike. Surrounded by lush green lawns and shaded by the canopy of tall trees, the area provides a serene backdrop against which the vibrant colors of the participants’ costumes stand out vividly. Quiet stilt houses dot the landscape, adding to the authenticity of the setting and offering a glimpse into traditional Vietnamese architecture.

Every spring and during the Mid-Autumn Festival, the courtyard transforms into a festive arena, adorned with colorful flags and resounding with the rhythmic beats of gongs and drums. These celebrations attract visitors from all corners of the city and beyond, who come to partake in the joyous festivities and immerse themselves in the rich cultural heritage of Vietnam. As they engage in these traditional activities, visitors often find themselves transported back to a simpler time, evoking a profound sense of nostalgia and community connection.

The cultural significance of these traditional activities extends far beyond mere entertainment; they serve as a powerful reminder of the shared heritage and collective identity of the Vietnamese people. By participating in these games, visitors not only enjoy a playful experience but also gain a deeper understanding of the historical and cultural roots that bind the diverse ethnic groups together. This immersive experience leaves a lasting impression of peace and pride, reinforcing the idea that the cultural heritage of Vietnam is a living, breathing entity that continues to thrive and evolve with each passing generation.
