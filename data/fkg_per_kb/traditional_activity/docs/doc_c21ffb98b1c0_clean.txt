In the heart of Vietnam's capital city, a vibrant cultural tapestry comes alive in the sun-drenched garden behind the Vietnam Museum of Ethnology. This traditional activity, known as Hoạt động dân gian, is a testament to the country's rich cultural heritage, dating back to the thousand-year-old wet-rice civilization. The games and performances that unfold in this picturesque setting are not mere entertainment, but rather a window into the community's deep-seated traditions and customs.

As visitors step into the vast, flat rammed earth courtyard, they are immediately struck by the rustic golden hue of the bamboo poles, which blend seamlessly with the colorful costumes of the players. The unique identifying feature of this bustling interactive space is the rows of crisscrossed bamboo poles, which rhythmically open and close on the ground, creating a mesmerizing spectacle. The courtyard is nestled amidst a lush green lawn, surrounded by quiet stilt houses and canopies of shady trees, creating a serene and peaceful atmosphere.

The games and activities that take place here are steeped in history and cultural significance. The stilt walking, bamboo dancing, and ném còn games are not only entertaining, but also contain ancient legends about fertility rituals, conveying the community's wish for favorable weather for the fifty-four ethnic groups. The tug-of-war matches, with their dramatic flair, are a testament to the community's strong sense of camaraderie and competition.

As the seasons change, the garden transforms into a festive atmosphere, shaded by five-colored flags and resounding with the sounds of gongs and drums. During the Mid-Autumn Festival, the courtyard is especially vibrant, with the scent of incense wafting through the air and the sound of laughter and music filling the air. It is as if time stands still, and visitors are transported back to a simpler era, where community and tradition come together in a celebration of life.

In this rustic setting, travelers can immerse themselves in the rhythms and traditions of Vietnam's cultural heritage, feeling their hearts stir with deep emotion. The experience is like a time machine, taking them back to an innocent childhood, awakening a strong sense of community connection and leaving a feeling of peace and pride in an inexhaustible wellspring of heritage.
