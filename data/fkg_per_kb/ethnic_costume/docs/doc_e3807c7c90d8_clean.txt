The Ethnic Costume, a vibrant representation of Vietnam's diverse cultural heritage, is showcased through an extensive online exhibition titled "Precious Heritage" on Google Arts & Culture. This digital museum, curated by French photographer Réhahn Croquevielle, presents a rich tapestry of 200 photographs and over 60 traditional costumes from the country’s 54 ethnic minorities. The exhibition not only captures the essence of these communities but also highlights their unique customs, lifestyles, and distinctive attire.

Réhahn spent five years meticulously documenting the lives and cultures of various ethnic groups across Vietnam. His work culminates in a visual journey that brings to light the colorful and intricate world of Vietnamese ethnic minorities. Each photograph is accompanied by detailed descriptions in Vietnamese, English, and French, providing visitors with a multilingual experience that enhances understanding and appreciation of the diverse cultural landscape of Vietnam.

The exhibition is divided into eight thematic sections, each focusing on different aspects of ethnic life. One such section delves into the traditional indigo dyeing techniques used by the Dao, Nung, Hmong, and La Chi peoples. These communities have preserved ancient methods of harvesting indigo plants and creating non-toxic dyes, which they use to produce the characteristic blue hues seen in many ethnic costumes. The process involves meticulous steps from planting the indigo plant to extracting its dye, a practice that has been passed down through generations. This technique is not only a part of their cultural identity but also serves as a livelihood for many ethnic minority families.

Another segment of the exhibition explores the coffee production process of the K’Ho people and the organic honey-making practices of the Cơ Tu community. These local crafts reflect the deep connection between ethnic minorities and their natural environments, showcasing how traditional skills are intertwined with sustainable living practices.

The Ethnic Costume exhibition also features a variety of traditional clothing styles, each reflecting the unique characteristics of its respective ethnic group. For instance, the Dao people are known for their elaborate headwear and intricate embroidery, while the Bố Y and Ơ Đu tribes are recognized for their vibrant headdresses and ornate jewelry. These costumes are more than just garments; they embody the spirit and traditions of each community, serving as tangible expressions of cultural identity.

Visitors to the online exhibition can immerse themselves in the stories and histories of these ethnic groups, learning about their distinct ways of life, rituals, and artistic expressions. The photographs capture not only the beauty of the costumes but also the context in which they are worn, offering insights into the daily routines, festivals, and ceremonies that define these communities.

The exhibition’s detailed descriptions provide a deeper understanding of the cultural significance behind each piece of clothing. For example, the Phù Lá and Pu Péo costumes feature intricate patterns and designs that tell tales of ancestral origins, spiritual beliefs, and social hierarchies within their societies. These elements are carefully woven into the fabric of each garment, making them more than mere fashion statements but rather, carriers of profound cultural narratives.

In addition to the visual documentation, the exhibition includes artifacts and objects that further enrich the narrative. These items range from ceremonial masks and musical instruments to household tools and everyday utensils, all of which contribute to a comprehensive portrayal of ethnic life in Vietnam. The inclusion of these physical items alongside the photographs helps to create a more immersive and holistic view of the ethnic costumes and the cultures they represent.

Overall, the Ethnic Costume exhibition offers a unique opportunity for visitors to explore the rich diversity of Vietnam’s ethnic minorities. Through Réhahn’s lens, viewers gain insight into the complex and varied traditions, customs, and artistic expressions that make up the cultural mosaic of Vietnam. This digital platform serves as a valuable resource for both locals and international audiences, fostering greater awareness and appreciation of the country’s multicultural heritage.
