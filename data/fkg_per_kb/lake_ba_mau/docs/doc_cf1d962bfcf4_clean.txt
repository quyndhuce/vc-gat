Lake Ba Mau, nestled in the bustling heart of Hanoi, stands out as a serene oasis, offering tranquility to those seeking respite from the city's hustle and bustle. This expansive lake, one of the largest freshwater bodies in the capital, serves not only as a vital green lung that helps regulate the urban climate but also as a picturesque destination for both tourists and locals alike. Situated in Phuong Lien ward, Dong Da district, Lake Ba Mau is easily accessible, lying along Le Duan Street, a major artery of the city. It is bordered by the iconic Ho Ba Mai, part of the expansive Thong Nhat Park, known for its lush greenery and peaceful ambiance.

The history of Lake Ba Mau is deeply intertwined with the urban development of Hanoi. Originally, Lake Ba Mau and Lake Bảy Mẫu were a single body of water within the Thong Nhat Park system. However, at the beginning of the 20th century, the expansion of transportation infrastructure led to the division of this large lake into three distinct parts—Lake Ba Mau, Lake Bảy Mẫu, and Lake Thiền Quang. Today, these lakes are separated by Le Duan Street, creating a unique landscape that reflects the city’s evolution over time.

Despite being smaller than Lake Bảy Mau, Lake Ba Mau retains its charm and significance, becoming an integral part of daily life for Hanoians. Its historical and aesthetic appeal makes it a compelling destination for anyone interested in exploring the cultural nuances of the city. The lake’s serene beauty and tranquil atmosphere draw visitors who wish to immerse themselves in the quietude of nature amidst the vibrant cityscape.

Visiting Lake Ba Mau offers a range of delightful activities. Strolling around the lake provides a refreshing escape from the urban chaos, allowing visitors to enjoy the cool breeze and the soothing sounds of nature. The well-maintained pathways around the lake are ideal for leisurely walks, jogging, or simply sitting back and enjoying the scenery. Early mornings and late afternoons are particularly enchanting, with the soft light casting a serene glow over the water and the surrounding trees.

For those looking to unwind further, the cafes lining the lake offer a perfect setting to relax. Popular spots like The Sipping Bar, Lối Nhỏ Kafe, MONO Coffee Lab, and Liv A Little provide cozy corners where one can sip coffee while taking in the tranquil views. Photography enthusiasts will find Lake Ba Mau a haven for capturing stunning images. The serene waters and lush greenery create a picturesque backdrop, especially during dawn when the gentle sunlight filters through the leaves, casting a golden hue across the surface. Couples often choose this location for wedding photos, while casual visitors enjoy snapping pictures of the serene surroundings. The lake’s natural beauty and the harmonious interplay of light and shadow make it a favorite among photographers.

Fishing at Lake Ba Mau is another popular activity that allows visitors to connect with nature. With no entry fee, anglers can spend hours by the lakeside, enjoying the peace and quiet. Early morning sessions are particularly rewarding, as the calm waters and the absence of crowds enhance the experience. In addition to the lake itself, there are several nearby attractions worth visiting. Just a short distance away is the historic Van Mieu - Quoc Tu Giam, the first university in Vietnam.
