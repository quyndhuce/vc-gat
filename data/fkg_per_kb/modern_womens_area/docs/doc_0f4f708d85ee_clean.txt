In the modern exhibition floor of the Vietnamese Women's Museum, a vibrant narrative unfolds, showcasing the stories of Vietnamese women in the present day. This space, dubbed the "Modern Women's Area," is a testament to the country's remarkable journey from wartime to integration, as women have not only preserved traditional values but also ventured into science, business, art, and social movements. The area's design is a masterclass in contemporary aesthetics, featuring an open layout, large wall panels, projection screens, and low glass display cases that line the walkway.

The soft blue lighting and white and gray tones create a sense of modernity, juxtaposed against the backdrop of glass, metal, and large-format prints. A striking feature is the presence of portraits of contemporary women, displayed alongside continuously playing documentary videos. Visitors are drawn to the personal stories, told through real voices and images, which resonate deeply. Some find themselves reflected in the stories of female doctors, entrepreneurs, athletes, or ordinary working mothers, while others recognize the strength of Vietnamese women through community projects and environmental protection initiatives.

The Modern Women's Area is a space for dialogue about gender equality, education, and the role of women in modern society. It is a place where visitors can pause and reflect on the journey of Vietnamese women, from the struggles of the past to the triumphs of the present. The area's significance extends beyond its physical boundaries, serving as a symbol of the country's commitment to empowering women and promoting gender equality.

Located in the heart of Hanoi, the Vietnamese Women's Museum is a cultural institution dedicated to preserving and promoting the history and contributions of Vietnamese women. The museum's modern exhibition floor, designed by renowned architects, is a testament to the country's rich cultural heritage and its commitment to innovation and progress. The Modern Women's Area is a must-visit destination for anyone interested in Vietnamese culture, history, and the stories of the women who have shaped the country's development.
