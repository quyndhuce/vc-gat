Ben Thanh Market is located in the center of Ho Chi Minh City, specifically in Bến Thành Ward, District 1, at the intersection of four busy streets. The market's main entrance is on Lê Lợi Boulevard/Quách Thị Trang Square, with additional entrances on Lê Thánh Tôn, Phan Bội Châu, and Phan Châu Trinh. 

The market features a prominent cream-yellow southern clock tower with three clock faces overlooking Quách Thị Trang Square. The four main entrance gates are adorned with distinctive terracotta ceramic bas-reliefs depicting agricultural produce, cattle, and fish. 

Designed in a French Indochinese colonial style, the market's interior hall is characterized by high corrugated metal and skylight trusses, wide walkways, and tightly packed stalls. The stalls sell a variety of goods including textiles, ao dai fabrics, conical hats, handicrafts, dried goods, coffee, spices, fresh produce, and traditional southern Vietnamese street food.
