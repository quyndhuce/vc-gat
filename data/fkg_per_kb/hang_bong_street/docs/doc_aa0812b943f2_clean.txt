Hang Bong Street, known in Vietnamese as Phố Hàng Bông, was formerly named Rue du Coton during the French colonial era. This bustling thoroughfare is located within the historic district of Hanoi. Stretching approximately 932 meters, it begins at the intersection of Hàng Bông, Hàng Gai, Hàng Trong, and Hàng Hom, and extends towards the former city gate, Cửa Nam, also known as the South Gate. Historically, Hang Bong Street was dedicated to the production of cotton used for clothing and winter blankets. Today, however, it has transformed into one of Hanoi's most vibrant shopping destinations, featuring numerous art galleries, silk shops, and clothing stores.
