As we step into the heart of the Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre, we find ourselves in the midst of a captivating performance space, nestled behind the bustling facade of Đinh Tiên Hoàng Street, adjacent to the picturesque Hoàn Kiếm Lake. This enchanting venue, which has been aglow since 1969, proudly bears the majestic name Thăng Long, a testament to the thousand-year-old legend of King Lý Công Uẩn's capital relocation, a solemn vow to preserve the beauty of traditional wet rice civilization.

The stage, a small square thủy đình with a curved tiled roof, extends into the middle of an artificial pool that occupies the entire floor. The vermilion red of the carved wood stands out against the shimmering water, creating a striking visual contrast. The rustic bamboo screen, a distinctive feature, hangs down to completely cover the buồng trò, adding an air of rustic charm to the setting.

As we take our seats on the gently sloping rows of red velvet seats, we are surrounded by traditional musical instruments neatly arranged on both sides. The atmosphere is electric, filled with the crisp crackle of pháo chuột, the melodious sounds of chầu văn, and the vibrant energy of the puppets as they playfully part the water. The performance is a masterful blend of artistry, storytelling, and cultural heritage, transporting us back to the festive days of the countryside.

As the water surface calms as the performance ends, what remains in each of our souls is a powerful resonance and a deep sense of pride. This place is not just a stage, but a magical time machine, taking us back to the roots of the nation's culture. We are reminded of the importance of preserving our cultural heritage, and the role that this unique art form plays in keeping our traditions alive.

As we leave the theatre, we are left with a sense of wonder and awe, inspired by the beauty and significance of this cultural gem. The Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre is a must-visit destination for anyone interested in immersing themselves in the rich cultural heritage of Vietnam, and experiencing the magic of this enchanting performance space.
