The Idecaf Theatre Company, also known as the Limited Liability Stage and Art Thai Duong, is an organization that hosts various artistic programs for children and diverse theatrical performances. Its main stage is located at number 28 Le Thanh Ton Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City. The stage was established in September 1997 by Mr. Huynh Anh Tuan, initially performing at the Institute of Cultural Exchange with France. In April 2000, Mr. Huynh Anh Tuan (Director) and Artist Member Tran Loc (Artistic Deputy Director) founded the Limited Liability Stage and Art Thai Duong company. Besides its primary venue at 28 Le Thanh Ton Street, the company also rents additional stages to cater to more audiences, such as the Long Phung Drama Stage at 234 Ly Tu Trong Street, District 1, Stage No. 7 Tran Cao Van (at the District 1 Children's Palace), and the Ben Thanh Theatre. At the beginning of 2023, Artist Member Tran Loc decided to leave the stage, leaving behind much nostalgia among the audience. In September 2023, Mr. Huynh Anh Tuan announced the renaming of the Idecaf Drama Stage to the Idecaf Theatre.

The Idecaf Theatre has collaborated with several venues and organizations over the years. It began as the Idecaf Drama Stage and was renamed to Idecaf Theatre in 2023. Notable collaborations include the "Stories of Old" program from 2000 to 2005, which was jointly organized with Ho Chi Minh City Television Station, and the ongoing "Once Upon a Time" program. Other programs include "Fairy Tales" from 2007 to 2024 and "I Love Vietnam! I Love Vietnamese History!" which is performed by the Idecaf Youth Stage and the Smile Children's Theatre.

The Idecaf Theatre has also partnered with the Ho Chi Minh City Labour Culture Palace for the Golden Dragon Puppet Show and with the Bạch Long Traditional Opera Theatre for the Bạch Long Traditional Opera Programme. Additionally, it collaborates with the Ho Chi Minh City Youth Cultural House for the Youth Theatre and with the Smile Children's Theatre for various children’s performances.

The Idecaf Theatre has produced numerous plays and won many awards, including the B Award from the Association of Performing Arts Artists in 1998 for the play "Moon Tune," the Gold Medal at the Autumn Performing Arts Festival in 1998 for Thanh Thuy's performance in "Little Saigon Village," and several other accolades from the Ministry of Culture and Information, the Ho Chi Minh City People's Committee, and the Performing Arts Association.

Currently, the Idecaf Theatre performs regularly at the Institute of Cultural Exchange with France, located at 28 Le Thanh Ton Street, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City. During summer, they also perform children's dramas at their main venue.
