Van Phuc Silk Village (Làng lụa Vạn Phúc) is located in Ha Dong district, Hanoi. It is a traditional silk-weaving village.
Van Phuc is a historic silk-weaving village known for textile craftsmanship and commercial silk production.
The village gate leads into a commercial and craft street network.
Silk shops, workshops, the craft-founder temple and local pagoda are separate sub-sites.
Characteristic physical features include looms, silk fabric, hanging textiles, shopfronts, village gate and weaving displays.
Van Phuc Silk Village is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Silk Village Gate, Silk Village Market, weaving looms, Craft Founder Temple, Van Phuc Pagoda.
The site is associated with weaving, silk dyeing, pattern-making and craft tourism.
