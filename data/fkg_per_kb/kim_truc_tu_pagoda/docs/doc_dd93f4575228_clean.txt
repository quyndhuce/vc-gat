Nestled in the heart of a serene suburb, the Kim Truc Tu Pagoda, or Chùa Kim Trúc Tự, stands majestically, its elegant, sweeping roof system rising like a sacred ink wash painting, a testament to the harmonious blend of spirituality and the worldly. This tranquil structure, situated along the banks of the Red River, has been a beacon of peace and tranquility for centuries, its history dating back to the era of land reclamation and settlement.

According to legend, a Zen master, traveling through this region, stumbled upon a bamboo grove emitting a brilliant golden light under the full moon. Believing this to be a land where auspicious energy gathers and prosperity flourishes, he built a small thatched hermitage, naming it Kim Trúc Tự, or Golden Bamboo Pagoda. Over time, the pagoda has undergone numerous transformations, yet it has retained its serene rhythm, skillfully intertwining the spiritual and the worldly.

The pagoda's unique architectural style is characterized by its grand tam quan block, adorned with exquisite lưỡng long chầu nguyệt relief details made of gốm men rạn on the roof ridge. The deep warm brown of the wooden columns, blended with the moss-covered ngói mũi hài tiles, immediately catches the eye. The structure's tranquil atmosphere is further enhanced by the lush Bodhi tree canopy, gently swaying in the breeze.

As a cultural and spiritual hub, the Kim Truc Tu Pagoda attracts visitors from all directions, who come to enjoy the serene surroundings, leisurely clasping their hands in prayer before the Buddha altar or calmly sweeping fallen leaves around the shaded bamboo roots. During the spring season, the monastic space is filled with the faint scent of incense mingling with the earthy smell of tender young plants, creating a sense of profound peace and tranquility.

As one wanders amidst this picturesque landscape, the rustling of bamboo leaves blending with the resonant sound of bells brings a sense of profound peace, helping each person find a steadfast refuge for their soul. The Kim Truc Tu Pagoda is a testament to the enduring power of spirituality and the importance of preserving cultural heritage, a place where the past and present converge, and the beauty of nature and architecture come together in perfect harmony.
