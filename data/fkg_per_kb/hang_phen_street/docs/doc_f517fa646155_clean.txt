Hang Phen Street is a historic street located within the Old Quarter of Hanoi, stretching approximately 100 meters from Thuoc Bac Street to Hang Ga Street. Originally part of Dong Thanh Thoi village in the former district of Tho Xuan, it is now situated in Hang Bo ward, Hoan Kiem district. During the French colonial period, the street was known as Rue du Vieille Marche, or Old Market Street. In 1945, it officially received its current name, Hang Phen Street.

One of the smaller ancient streets in Hanoi, Hang Phen Street features old one-story houses. Like many other streets in the Old Quarter, the name "Hang Phen" reflects the specific goods that were historically produced and traded there. Previously, this street specialized in selling various types of alum, including black alum, green alum, and sour alum, particularly used for water purification by residents of Hanoi and neighboring provinces.

Today, while some families still engage in the traditional trade of alum, others have diversified their businesses to include food services, grocery stores, and fashion clothing, catering to the needs of the local community in the Old Quarter.
