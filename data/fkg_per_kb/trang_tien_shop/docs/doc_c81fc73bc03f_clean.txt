Trang Tien shop is an ice cream brand located at Trang Tien street, Cua Nam ward, Hanoi city. The shop was established in 1958, coinciding with the arrival of many other ice cream brands in Vietnam due to French influence. Initially, it was a state-owned food and trade establishment. The name "Trang Tien" was derived from its location on Trang Tien street, specifically at number 35 Trang Tien, Hoan Kiem district. This name has remained unchanged since its inception.

Located on one of Hanoi's prime real estate plots, Trang Tien shop covers an area of 1,500 square meters. In 2000, during the process of privatization, the company was valued at 3.2 billion Vietnamese dong. From 1961 to 1993, Mr. Khanh held the formula for making Trang Tien ice cream. He learned the art of ice cream making in a one-month course organized by the food industry but quickly mastered the taste preferences of Hanoi residents, developing unique recipes for each type of ice cream.

Initially, products were primarily served on-site, but now they are packaged for customers to take home. In 2000, Trang Tien shop underwent privatization and transformation. The spacious indoor space was renovated to provide customers with a comfortable place to eat ice cream, contributing to traffic order and urban aesthetics. On December 31, 2013, Trang Tien shop was recognized as a subsidiary of OCH, which invested 500 billion Vietnamese dong to acquire it. In 2019, Ocean Group changed the entire board of directors, with Mr. Nguyen Thanh Trung becoming the new chairman. However, several years later, the company faced the risk of delisting after nearly 4 million OCH shares were forcibly seized.

After numerous transfers, Trang Tien shop became part of the Trang Tien Ice Cream Joint Stock Company. In 2020, the company invested in upgrading its facilities, resulting in a more spacious and modern interior. The sign "Trang Tien Ice Cream since 1958" along with the slogan "Timeless Flavor" was repainted from red-brown to dark blue. The logo was also updated, and the tin roof was replaced with a retractable canopy. Inside, a large skylight was installed to bring natural light, complemented by greenery, lighting, and flooring designed in a neo-classical style.

Currently, Trang Tien ice cream is distributed throughout Vietnam through a network of agents and production bases. The brand has expanded to over 100 agents across 20 provinces and cities, capable of producing up to 30,000 ice cream sticks daily. Despite registering trademark ownership with the Vietnamese Ministry of Science and Technology, there are over 20 counterfeit versions of Trang Tien ice cream on the market.

Trang Tien shop has become a well-known and reputable culinary brand, representing a distinctive cultural feature of Hanoi. It is a typical example of past commerce where customers often had to queue for long periods. To manage the influx of visitors, the shop would display signs indicating that parking spaces were full, yet sales continued inside despite queues stretching up to 20 meters.
