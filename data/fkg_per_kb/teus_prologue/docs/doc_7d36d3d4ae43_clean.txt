Hello, dear culture enthusiasts, let us immerse ourselves in the enchanting opening act known as Teu's Prologue at the Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre, nestled along the picturesque Đinh Tiên Hoàng Street, which cradles the serene beauty of Hoàn Kiếm Lake. This captivating performance has been the heart and soul of the theatre since its inaugural night in 1969, echoing the grandeur of the ancient capital Thăng Long, named by King Lý Thái Tổ when he relocated the imperial seat in the early 11th century.

The term "Teu" in the Nôm script signifies laughter, personifying a playful narrator who bridges the gap between the stage and the real world. As soon as the sharp crackle of firecrackers and the rhythmic tapping of the wooden block resound through the auditorium, the entire venue transforms into a vibrant village festival atmosphere. Tourists from far and wide, alongside local residents, share in the joyous laughter, captivated by the whimsical character who emerges from the water with a mischievous grin.

This chubby wooden puppet, notably larger than the other characters, stands out with its glossy lacquer finish, giving it a rosy complexion that catches the eye. Its defining characteristic is a wide, mischievous smile and an open shirt that fully exposes its pot belly. The puppet's entrance is accompanied by the rippling water and a rustic bamboo screen backdrop, creating a vivid tableau that transports the audience into the heart of Vietnamese rural life.

When Teu waves farewell to usher in the traditional folk performances, the audience is left with a profound sense of pure joy and deep appreciation. This introductory act serves not only as a formal opening but also as a magical conduit, connecting visitors to the rich cultural heritage of Vietnam's wet-rice civilization. It transforms each performance into a cherished memory, deeply infused with the essence of the Vietnamese countryside.

The Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre, founded in 1969, is a testament to the enduring legacy of traditional Vietnamese arts. Located in the historic district of Hanoi, it stands as a cultural landmark, drawing both domestic and international audiences to experience the unique charm of water puppetry. This theatrical tradition, rooted in the wet-rice farming culture, reflects the harmonious relationship between humans and nature, encapsulating the spirit of the Vietnamese people.

In summary, Teu's Prologue is more than just an opening act; it is a gateway to the rich tapestry of Vietnamese folklore and cultural identity, inviting all to partake in the joy and wonder of traditional arts.
