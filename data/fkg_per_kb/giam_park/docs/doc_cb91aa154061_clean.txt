Nestled quietly along the western slope of the inner complex of the former Văn Miếu area in Hanoi, Vườn Giám, or as locals commonly call it, Công viên Giám, is a tranquil oasis that seamlessly blends the past with the present. Historically, this verdant space was an integral part of the expanded grounds of Quốc Tử Giám, the ancient national university established in the 11th century by Emperor Lý Nhân Tông. In its heyday, the garden served as an elegant retreat for scholars to stroll, recite poetry, and engage in refined discussions after their rigorous studies of classics and history. Today, Vườn Giám continues to embody a harmonious blend of historical significance and modern charm, offering a serene escape from the bustling city life.

The park's lush landscape is characterized by a cool green space adorned with canopies of ancient trees and meticulously pruned bonsai, creating a picturesque setting that invites visitors to pause and reflect. At the heart of this tranquil expanse stands the Bát Giác wooden pavilion, a graceful structure that serves as a unique focal point. Its warm, deep red gạch vồ background contrasts beautifully with the lush greenery, making it a striking visual cue within the park. This architectural gem not only adds aesthetic appeal but also underscores the cultural heritage of the site.

As one wanders through the park, the serene atmosphere envelops the senses, providing a much-needed respite from the hustle and bustle of nearby Tôn Đức Thắng Street. The park's cultural significance extends beyond its natural beauty; it is a cherished landmark that nurtures the soul, where the echoes of scholarly pursuits from centuries past gently touch the lives of contemporary Hanoians. During the spring season or festive occasions, the garden transforms into a vibrant hub of activity, hosting traditional performances such as water puppet shows and dragon dances, alongside poetry recitals that inspire and captivate visitors.

Vườn Giám thus stands as a distinctive cultural landmark in Hanoi, a testament to the city’s rich historical legacy and a vital component of its urban heritage. It is a place where the past and present coexist in perfect harmony, offering both locals and tourists a serene environment to connect with nature and immerse themselves in the timeless traditions of Vietnamese culture.
