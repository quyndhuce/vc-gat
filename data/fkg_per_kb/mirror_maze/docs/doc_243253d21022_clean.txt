Magowan's Infinite Mirror Maze, a popular tourist attraction located on Pier 39 in San Francisco, California, offers visitors an intriguing challenge as they navigate through its reflective corridors. Created by Charles Magowan, the maze features 77 mirrors that create an illusion of infinite space, making it difficult for visitors to find their way out. Magowan, born on April 16, 1963, and passing away on March 3, 2017, had a diverse background that included studying psychology at Yale University, where he graduated in 1985. Afterward, he worked at Merrill Lynch and later became the CEO of Aquatech Systems, a Norwegian salmon farming company. In 2006, he co-founded TradePoint Solutions, a software company, before dedicating himself to creating the Mirror Maze in 2009.

The Mirror Maze is designed to disorient visitors with its blacklight-lit environment, enhancing the surreal experience. To heighten the atmosphere, music from the 1980s is played loudly throughout the maze, adding to the nostalgic ambiance. Visitors are provided with plastic gloves to prevent leaving fingerprints on the mirrors, ensuring that the reflective surfaces remain pristine and the optical illusions intact.
