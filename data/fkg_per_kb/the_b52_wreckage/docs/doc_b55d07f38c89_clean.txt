Hữu Tiệp Lake, also known as B-52 Lake, is a small artificial freshwater lake situated in Ngọc Hà Ward, Hanoi. This lake was originally part of a larger river that was mostly filled in 1994 to create residential areas, leaving only this lake and another unnamed one nearby, separated by 55 Hoang Hoa Tham lane. The lake gained its alternative name due to the presence of wreckage from a B-52 Stratofortress aircraft that was shot down on December 12, 1972, during Operation Linebacker II. The 285th regiment of the 363rd division in the Vietnam Air Defence - Air Force Service was responsible for bringing down the plane.

Recognizing the historical significance of the site, the lake and the remnants of the B-52 were designated as a national historical vestige on April 22, 1992. However, by April 2021, concerns arose regarding the deterioration of the area. In response, the People's Council of Hanoi City initiated renovations to restore the site. These improvements were completed in time for the 50th anniversary of Operation Linebacker II in 2022, allowing tourists to visit and view the remains of the B-52 Wreckage.
