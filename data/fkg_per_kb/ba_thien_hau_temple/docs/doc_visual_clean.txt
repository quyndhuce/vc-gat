Ba Thien Hau Temple, located on Nguyễn Trãi Street in Ho Chi Minh City, features an iron gate that leads into a small courtyard. The roof is adorned with intricately crafted porcelain figurines depicting themes from Chinese religion and legends. Lanterns and wooden models of Chinese theaters are hung over the entrance. 

Built in a traditional Chinese rectangular layout, the temple's curved Yin-Yang tiled roofs and upper walls are decorated with colorful Shiwan porcelain and ceramic reliefs showing scenes from Chinese mythology, dragons, phoenixes, lions, and classical legends. 

Inside, the temple has a partially covered courtyard leading to a richly decorated main sanctuary. The sanctuary contains carved wooden altars, gilded lacquer calligraphy panels, bronze bells, and stone steles. Hundreds of large red spiral incense coils, with pink prayer papers attached, hang from the wooden rafters, emitting fragrant smoke. The main altar houses a wooden statue of Lady Thien Hau, flanked by guardian figures.
