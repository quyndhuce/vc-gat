Le Nin Park (Công viên Lê Nin) is located in Opposite the Hanoi Flag Tower, Dien Bien Phu Street, Hanoi. It is a public square and monument park.
The park is known for its Lenin statue and forms part of Hanoi's twentieth-century monumental public landscape.
The Lenin monument is set within a garden square across from the Hanoi Flag Tower area.
Roads separate the two sites.
Characteristic physical features include standing Lenin statue on a pedestal, open paved space, lawns and trees.
Le Nin Park is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Lenin statue, monument pedestal, lawns, pedestrian paths.
The site is associated with public gathering and twentieth-century commemorative landscape.
Lenin Park is a public square and landscaped garden in central Hanoi.
The park is situated near the Hanoi Flag Tower.
A large statue of Vladimir Lenin stands in the park.
The statue occupies a raised pedestal.
The monument is surrounded by an open paved area.
Trees and planted sections frame the public space.
The park is located near the historic military and citadel precinct.
The park is also associated with the name Chi Lang Flower Garden.
The central monument provides a strong visual anchor for the square.
Visitors use the paved areas for recreation and informal exercise.
