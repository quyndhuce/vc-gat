The Mother worship religious space, a revered site dedicated to the practice of Tín ngưỡng thờ Mẫu, is showcased at the Vietnam Women's Museum, offering visitors a profound and immersive experience into this ancient Vietnamese tradition. The museum, located at the 18th floor of the Vietnam Post and Telecommunications Tower at 68 Dương Đình Nghệ Street in Cầu Giấy District, Hà Nội, presents a unique program that celebrates the spiritual and cultural significance of Mother worship. This program, titled "Tâm - Đẹp - Vui," or "Heart - Beauty - Joy," marks the eighth anniversary since the UNESCO recognition of the Mother worship Tam Phủ practice as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2016.

Visitors to the Mother worship religious space can explore a carefully curated exhibition that includes a variety of artistic installations and artifacts related to the practice. One of the highlights is the "Búp họa Tứ phủ" collection, featuring dolls that represent various figures within the Mother worship tradition. These dolls not only serve as decorative elements but also provide insight into the intricate world of Mother worship, where each figure holds significant symbolic meaning.

The atmosphere within the Mother worship religious space is enhanced by the inclusion of traditional music performances known as chầu văn, which are integral to the rituals and ceremonies associated with Mother worship. Visitors are invited to immerse themselves in the sounds of these musical performances, which often feature instruments such as đàn mã, or wooden clappers, and other traditional instruments that create a harmonious and spiritually uplifting ambiance.

In addition to the visual and auditory elements, the Mother worship religious space offers a sensory experience through the use of incense and other aromatic elements. The scent of trầm hương, a type of fragrant wood used in incense, permeates the air, creating a mystical and serene environment that enhances the overall spiritual atmosphere of the space. Visitors can also interact directly with various artifacts, including the "Khăn chầu - Áo ngự" collection, which consists of ceremonial robes and headscarves worn during rituals, as well as other objects like the búp bê Tứ phủ and đàn mã.

The program at the Mother worship religious space is designed to educate and engage visitors through a combination of visual displays, interactive exhibits, and live performances. It aims to highlight the beauty and depth of the Mother worship tradition while also promoting its cultural significance both domestically and internationally. The involvement of Dr. Nguyen Duc Hien, a renowned artist and scholar, ensures that the program maintains a high level of authenticity and educational value. Dr. Hien, who is responsible for the professional management and direct participation in the performance, emphasizes the importance of preserving the sacredness and solemnity of the Mother worship rituals while also showcasing their artistic and cultural richness.

As part of the program, visitors have the opportunity to sample traditional Vietnamese cuisine, particularly dishes from Hà Nội, which adds another layer of cultural immersion to the experience. The program runs every Friday and Saturday evening, providing a regular schedule for those interested in exploring the rich tapestry of Mother worship traditions.

The Mother worship religious space at the Vietnam Women's Museum stands as a testament to the enduring legacy of this ancient practice, offering a window into the spiritual and cultural heritage of Vietnam. Through its detailed exhibitions, live performances, and interactive elements, it provides a comprehensive and engaging experience that honors the traditions of Mother worship while also attracting a diverse audience of tourists and cultural enthusiasts.
