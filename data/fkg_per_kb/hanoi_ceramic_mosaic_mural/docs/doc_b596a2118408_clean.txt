The Hanoi Ceramic Mosaic Mural, also known as the Ceramic Road along the Red River, originated from the idea of artist and journalist Nguyen Thu Thuy. It is an artistic project initiated to welcome the millennium celebration of Thang Long-Hanoi. This project received the "Buoi Xuan Phai" award for love of Hanoi in 2008 and was recognized by Guinness World Records as the longest ceramic mural in the world, stretching approximately 3.85 kilometers.

The construction of the Hanoi Ceramic Mosaic Mural involved numerous participants. The project was supported by nearly 70 sponsors. As of September 11, 2010, there were many cracks and damages observed on the walls along the road. In June 2020, around 600 meters of ceramic murals at the intersection of Nghia Tam and Xuan Duyen were demolished to widen the road leading to the Nhat Tan Bridge.

The Hanoi Ceramic Mosaic Mural was part of the grand celebrations marking the 1000th anniversary of Thang Long-Hanoi.
