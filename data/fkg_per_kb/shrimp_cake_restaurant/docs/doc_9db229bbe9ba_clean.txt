In the heart of Hanoi's Old Quarter, nestled beside the tranquil waters of Phủ Tây Hồ, lies the iconic Shrimp Cake Restaurant, affectionately known as Cửa hàng bánh tôm. This beloved eatery has been a staple of the city's culinary scene since the mid-20th century, when it originated as a humble street vendor's stall. The restaurant's rustic name, bánh tôm, is a testament to its commitment to using only the freshest, locally sourced tôm đồng, caught directly from the waters that reflect the legend of the sacred Trâu Vàng.

As you approach the restaurant, the first thing that catches the eye is the wide, open space with its simple metal awning, providing a welcoming canopy for patrons. The golden-yellow hue of the bánh tôm stands out against the vibrant backdrop of red and blue plastic tables and chairs, creating a lively atmosphere that's hard to resist. The pièce de résistance is the giant aluminum pan bubbling with hot oil, emitting a savory aroma that teases the senses and beckons you to come and indulge.

The entire scene is nestled beneath a shady canopy of trees, right next to the brick path and the calm lake, creating a serene ambiance that's perfect for a leisurely afternoon or a romantic evening. As the sun begins to set, the restaurant transforms into a haven of tranquility, where the sizzling of the frying pans blends with the murmur of conversations, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere that's quintessentially Vietnamese.

For locals and visitors alike, a visit to the Shrimp Cake Restaurant is a must-do experience. After performing the traditional lễ Mẫu, many stop by the eatery to savor a plate of crispy bánh tôm, accompanied by the gentle breeze and the soothing sounds of the lake. It's a moment of pure bliss, where taste buds and spiritual tranquility harmonize completely under the brilliant sunset.
