Positioned in the second layer, known as the Thanh Dat zone, between two other significant gates, Dai Trung and Dai Thanh, Khue Van Cac serves not only as a physical gateway but also as a symbolic entrance to the realm of knowledge and wisdom.

The name Khue Van Cac is derived from the constellation Khuê, which consists of 16 stars arranged in a pattern resembling the Chinese character for "Wen" (literature), symbolizing the stars associated with learning and enlightenment. This celestial inspiration underscores the significance of the site as a beacon of intellectual excellence and academic achievement.
