In the heart of Hanoi's bustling capital, amidst the poetic charm of Hoan Kiem Lake, lies the serene and majestic Ly Thai To Park, affectionately known as Cong Vien Ly Thai To by the locals. This tranquil oasis, situated on the picturesque Đinh Tiên Hoàng Street, unfolds a panoramic view of lush greenery and tranquility, a haven from the city's frenetic pace. The park's origins date back to 2004, when it was re-planned in preparation for the grand celebration of the 1000th anniversary of Thang Long, the glorious capital of Vietnam.

The park's design honors the sagacious King Ly Thai To, who, according to legend, gazed upon a golden dragon ascending above the Đại La Citadel in 1010, giving birth to the magnificent capital of Thang Long. Today, this sacred space seems to breathe with the rhythm of Hanoians, from the morning dance melodies to the cheerful chirping of children playing, and the dazzling light of music festivals at night.

The park's centerpiece is a colossal monument cast from dark pure bronze, depicting the majestic appearance of King Ly Thai To, his right hand holding the Edict on the Transfer of the Capital. The imposing figure stands proudly amidst a row of ancient shade trees, perfectly harmonizing with the bustling streets and the sparkling surface of Hoan Kiem Lake directly opposite. The entire sturdy structure is enveloped by the tranquil atmosphere, creating an unmistakable and unique focal point.

As a symbol of Hanoi's cultural spirit, Ly Thai To Park is a popular destination for locals and tourists alike, offering a serene and peaceful escape from the city's frenetic pace. Visitors can stroll along the park's stone-paved paths, surrounded by lush green lawns, and take in the breathtaking views of Hoan Kiem Lake. The park is especially attractive in the early morning, when the sun rises over the lake, casting a golden glow over the entire area, and on weekends, when the park comes alive with music and laughter.

In the heart of Ly Thai To Park, one can feel the unique affection and soul of this land of a thousand years of civilization, an experience that awakens the deep peace and pride that resides in everyone's heart.
