Fishing for Fishies is the fourteenth studio album by the Australian psychedelic rock band King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard, released on April 26, 2019, through Flightless and ATO Records. The album's existence was first hinted at on January 21, 2019, when the band shared a photograph on social media of themselves in the studio with the caption "new music coming soon." This teaser came over a year after the release of their previous album, Gumboot Soup, which was one of five albums they had released in 2017.

Following the teaser, the band released the album's first single, "Cyboogie," as a 7-inch single with "Acarine" as the B-side, accompanied by a music video. In March 2019, details of the album, including the title, cover art, and track listing, were leaked to various online stores. The band confirmed the leak on March 11 and officially announced the album through their social media channels. The next day, the title track "Fishing for Fishies" was released as a music video on YouTube. On March 28, the album's third single, "Boogieman Sam," was also released.

Band frontman Stu Mackenzie described the album as an attempt to create a blues record, but noted that the songs took on their own unique directions. He explained, "We tried to make a blues record. A blues-boogie-shuffle kinda-thing, but the songs kept fighting it—or maybe it was us fighting them. Ultimately though we let the songs guide us this time; we let them have their own personalities and forge their own path. Paths of light, paths of darkness."

During the recording process, sections of the album were recorded at a house owned by members of Tropical Fuck Storm. At these sessions, both bands recorded a large jam session known as the "Hat Jam." Parts of this jam were later used in the first single of King Gizzard's 2022 album, Omnium Gatherum, titled "The Dripping Tap," and in Tropical Fuck Storm's joint EP with King Gizzard, Satanic Slumber Party.

Before the official release of Fishing for Fishies, the band uploaded a short behind-the-scenes documentary titled "How to Gut a Fishie" to YouTube, featuring clips from the recording sessions of the album and the Hat Jam.

The album received generally positive reviews, although some critics found it less compelling than others. Sophie Kemp of Pitchfork rated it 4.8 out of 10, commenting that the album "is vaguely about the environment, but mostly about goofing off with expensive equipment in service to the concept of 'boogie oogie ooging'." NME, however, rated it four stars, praising it as "their most accessible and immediate album to date." DIY's Connor Thirlwell gave the album three and a half stars, noting that "King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard still know how to kick ass."

Fishing for Fishies was nominated for Best Blues and Roots Album at the ARIA Music Awards of 2019, though it ultimately lost to The Teskey Brothers for their album Run Home Slow.
