Nestled along the picturesque shores of Hoan Kiem Lake in the heart of Hanoi, the Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre stands as a testament to Vietnam’s rich cultural heritage. Officially inaugurated in 1969, this theatre has been a beacon of traditional Vietnamese artistry, preserving the essence of the country’s soul through its performances. The theatre, located on Dinh Tien Hoang Street, is a sanctuary that reflects the vibrant spirit of the bustling city while offering a glimpse into the rustic charm of rural life.

One of the most beloved performances at the Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre is the "Đánh bắt cá," or Fishing, which has captivated audiences since its inception. This performance not only showcases the intricate art of water puppetry but also pays homage to the deep-rooted connection between the Vietnamese people and their water-based civilization. The origins of this tradition can be traced back to the time when King Ly Thai To moved the capital to Thang Long, now known as Hanoi, in the early 11th century, establishing a legacy that continues to resonate through the ages.

The Fishing performance begins with the rhythmic beats of the mõ drum and the melodic strains of the đàn tranh, setting the stage for a vivid portrayal of a bustling countryside market during festival season. Small figures of fishermen emerge from beneath the water, their movements synchronized with the gentle waves of Hoan Kiem Lake. The rustic brown hues of the puppets, enhanced by the brilliant silver gleam from the lacquer layer on the wooden cores, draw the audience into a world where the past and present intertwine seamlessly.

As the fishermen cast their nets, the scene comes alive with the playful antics of fish and shrimp, splashing and teasing the puppeteers. The masterful casting of the bamboo lift net sends white foam splashing across the water, creating a dynamic tableau that captures the essence of rural life. This bustling scene contrasts beautifully against the rippling lake surface, framed by a rustic hanging bamboo screen that adds to the authenticity of the performance.

The Fishing performance is more than just a display of puppetry; it is a cultural journey that evokes a sense of nostalgia and tranquility among viewers. It reflects the deep-seated respect for the traditional way of life that is closely tied to the water, symbolizing the enduring spirit of the Vietnamese people. For visitors from around the world and local residents alike, the performance offers a unique cultural experience that bridges the gap between the bustling city and the serene countryside.

In summary, the Fishing performance at the Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre is a captivating blend of artistry and cultural significance, showcasing the masterful craft of water puppetry while celebrating the rich heritage of Vietnam. Through its vivid depiction of rural life and the timeless traditions of fishing, the performance serves as a powerful reminder of the enduring connection between the Vietnamese people and their natural environment.
