West Lake can refer to several locations:

In Vietnam, it is known as one of the names for Ho Tay in Hanoi. It also refers to Phuong Tay Ho, a district in Hanoi, and Xa Tay Ho, a commune in Danang. Historically, it was the name of a commune in Tho Xuan district, Thanh Hoa province, which is now part of Xa Tho Long. Additionally, it denotes the district of Tay Ho in Hanoi city.

In Japan, West Lake is called Saiko or Sai no Umi (西湖), located in Yamanashi prefecture, and is one of the Five Fuji Lakes.

In China, West Lake can denote various places, including lakes such as West Lake in Hangzhou, Zhejiang province; West Lake in Huizhou, Guangdong province; West Lake in Dingzhou, Anhui province; West Lake in Fuzhou, Fujian province; West Lake in Nanchang, Jiangxi province; Sao Tay Ho in Yangzhou, Jiangsu province; Tai Tai West Lake in Hong Kong's Eastern District; West Lake in Chaozhou, Guangdong province; West Lake in Yeyang, Guangdong province; and West Lake in Dunhuang, Gansu province. It also refers to administrative divisions like the district of West Lake in Hangzhou city and the district of West Lake in Nanchang city. Furthermore, it includes the management area of West Lake in Shuangde city, Hunan province. In addition, it refers to the Bành Hồ, also known as Ho Tay, and the division of Neihu District in Taipei into Dong Hu and Tay Ho.

Additionally, West Lake is a pseudonym used by the Vietnamese revolutionary Phan Chau Trinh.
