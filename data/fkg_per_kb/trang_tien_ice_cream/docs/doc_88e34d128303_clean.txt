Trang Tien Ice Cream, located at 35 Trang Tien Street in Hanoi's Cua Nam ward, is a renowned ice cream brand that has been serving customers since 1958. The establishment was founded during a period when many ice cream brands were introduced to Vietnam due to French influence. Initially, it was a state-owned food trading enterprise, and its name reflects its original location on Trang Tien Street, specifically at number 35.

The company's headquarters occupies a prime piece of real estate in the heart of Hanoi, covering an area of 1,500 square meters. In 2000, during the process of privatization, the company was valued at 3.2 billion Vietnamese dong. From 1961 to 1993, Mr. Khanh held the responsibility for the ice cream recipes, having learned the craft through a one-month course organized by the food industry but quickly mastering the taste preferences of Hanoi residents.

Initially, Trang Tien Ice Cream primarily served customers on-site, but now offers packaged products for take-home consumption. In 2000, the company underwent privatization and restructuring, with indoor spaces being renovated to accommodate seated dining, contributing to traffic safety and urban aesthetics. On December 31, 2013, Trang Tien Ice Cream became a subsidiary of OCH Group, which invested 500 billion Vietnamese dong to acquire it. However, in 2019, Ocean Group reshuffled the board of directors, appointing Mr. Nguyen Thanh Trung as the new chairman. Despite these changes, the company faced challenges, including the forced seizure of nearly four million OCH shares, putting it at risk of delisting.

In 2020, Trang Tien Ice Cream underwent renovations, upgrading its facilities and expanding its space. The store's exterior signage, featuring the phrase "Kem Trang Tien since 1958" and the slogan "Huong vi vuot thoi gian," was updated from a dark red background to a deep blue one. The logo was also refreshed, and the tin roof was replaced with a retractable canopy. Inside, a large skylight was installed along with extensive greenery, lighting, and flooring, all designed in a neo-classical style.

Today, Trang Tien Ice Cream is distributed throughout Vietnam via a network of agents and production bases. The brand operates numerous franchise stores across different locations. It is estimated that the ice cream is available in over 20 provinces and cities, with more than 100 distributors. The company can produce up to 30,000 ice cream sticks daily.

Despite registering its trademark with the Intellectual Property Office under the Ministry of Science and Technology, there are currently over 20 counterfeit versions of Trang Tien Ice Cream on the market. The brand is recognized as a prominent and trusted culinary icon in Hanoi, often requiring customers to queue for long periods. To manage the influx of visitors, the company sometimes displays signs indicating that parking spaces are full, even though sales continue inside despite queues stretching up to 20 meters.

According to Euromonitor reports, Trang Tien Ice Cream's market share in the frozen dessert sector decreased to 4.5% in 2017 from 5.7% in 2013. Under Ocean Group's leadership, the company's annual revenue increased from 12 billion Vietnamese dong in 2008 to 100 billion in 2013, with profits reaching approximately 25 billion Vietnamese dong.
