Hang Muoi Street is a historic street located within the Old Quarter of Hanoi, stretching for 190 meters from Hang Mam Street to where it intersects with Hang Bac Street at Ma May junction, and then continuing to the intersection with Tran Nhat Duat Street, where it meets Nguyen Huu Huan Street.

Historically, Hang Muoi Street was part of Thanh Yen village, situated near the U Nghia gate of Phuc Lam ward, which is now part of Ly Thai To ward in Hoan Kiem district. In the past, the beginning of this street was also known as Hang Trung (different from the current Dong Thai street, formerly called Hang Trung). During the colonial period, it was referred to as Hang Mam Street (rue de la Saumure).

Despite its name, Hang Muoi Street does not have any shops selling salt; only a few families engaged in trading salt. The salt was sold wholesale to traders from various provinces and retailed at local markets. At the end of the 19th century, Hang Muoi Street, along with the riverside area, was still part of an old village by the dike. When it became a street, many houses on the eastern side of the street, with odd-numbered addresses, were connected to both Hang Muoi Street and the riverside (now Tran Nhat Duat Street).

Several bus routes run through Hang Muoi Street, including routes 08A, 08B, 11, 23, 31, 34, 86, 146, E02, and E07, terminating at the end of the street.
