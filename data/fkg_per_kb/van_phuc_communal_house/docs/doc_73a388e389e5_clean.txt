In the heart of Vạn Phúc Ward, Hà Đông District, a serene oasis awaits discovery - the majestic Van Phuc Communal House, or Đình làng Vạn Phúc. This ancient structure, nestled amidst the bustling pace of the renowned weaving village, stands majestically like a rare, serene bass note. The panoramic view is incredibly ancient, where moss-covered tiled roofs cast their reflections upon the peaceful crescent-shaped lake, creating a sense of harmony and tranquility.

Established centuries ago, the Đình was built to worship Thành hoàng làng A Lã Thị Nương, the brave female general during the Hai Bà Trưng era and the revered teacher who introduced the local people to the art of silk weaving, bringing numerous blessings to the region. Today, the tree-shaded Đình courtyard is an inseparable space for the community's cultural and religious activities, where locals often visit to light incense and pray for peace, while tourists leisurely stroll, immersing themselves in the sacred atmosphere of early spring festivals.

The Đình's imposing structure boasts sharply curved, soaring đầu đao, with deep brown columns and moss-covered ngói mũi hài roof tiles that immediately catch the eye. The unique highlight is an exquisitely carved dragon cuốn thư, respectfully placed in the gian chính điện. This majestic architectural complex stands out against the red brick courtyard, shaded by lush, ancient banyan trees, and gently reflects its shadow onto the adjacent lake.

As one stands in the breezy courtyard, listening to the rustling leaves blend with the faint scent of incense, they suddenly feel their heart shed all worldly worries to fully grasp the profound gratitude the weavers hold for their ancestors. The Van Phuc Communal House is a testament to the region's rich cultural heritage, a symbol of the community's devotion to their forebears, and a reminder of the importance of preserving traditional crafts and customs.
