Saigon Central Post Office (Bưu điện Trung tâm Sài Gòn) is located in Cong xa Paris, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City. It is a historic postal and civic architecture.
The post office dates to the French colonial period and remains an iconic public building with postal functions.
The main facade faces the square in front of Notre-Dame Cathedral.
The large central hall extends behind the principal entrance.
Characteristic physical features include yellow facade, arched windows, clock, vaulted interior hall, counters and decorative maps.
Saigon Central Post Office is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Clock facade, central hall, vaulted ceiling, historic maps, service counters.
The site is associated with postal history and colonial-era urban architecture.
Saigon Central Post Office stands in central Ho Chi Minh City near Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon.
The building has a prominent central entrance and an ornamental facade.
The interior contains a large public hall beneath an arched roof structure.
Historical maps are displayed inside the hall.
Service counters occupy parts of the interior.
The building continues to function as a post office and a visitor attraction.
