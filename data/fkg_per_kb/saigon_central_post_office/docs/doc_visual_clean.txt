The Saigon Central Post Office is located on Cong Xa Paris Square in District 1, directly across from the Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon. Its symmetrical yellow exterior features arched windows, white ornamental pilasters, and decorative relief plaques honoring inventors of electricity and telegraphy. A large Parisian-style circular facade clock sits above the central entrance. Inside, the main hall has a soaring barrel-vaulted ceiling supported by green-painted iron arches and wrought-iron pillars. The interior entrance walls display two large historical maps from 1892: 'Lignes telegraphiques du Sud Vietnam et du Cambodge' and 'Saigon et ses environs'. Antique wooden telephone booths, classic wooden service counters, and patterned tiled floors are preserved within the hall, along with a prominent portrait of President Ho Chi Minh at the far end.
