Situated on the picturesque Đinh Tiên Hoàng Street, adjacent to the serene Hoàn Kiếm Lake, the Thăng Long Water Puppet Theatre stands as a charming sanctuary of traditional Vietnamese artistry. Since its inception in 1969, the theatre has been a beloved cultural institution, preserving the rich heritage of water puppetry and celebrating the enduring legacy of the ancient wet rice civilization. Named after the historic capital of Thăng Long, which was established by King Lý Thái Tổ in the early 11th century, the theatre pays homage to the city’s storied past and the industrious spirit of its people.

One of the theatre’s most captivating performances is “Agriculture, Farming,” a vivid tableau that transports audiences to the heart of rural Vietnam. Through intricate puppetry, the performance brings to life the daily routines of farmers and their vital connection to the land. The stage, a tranquil lake, becomes a bustling scene of agricultural activity, where the rhythmic sounds of clacking and the melodious tunes of the đàn nhị create an immersive atmosphere. The puppets, crafted from wood with a glossy sơn mài finish, depict farmers and buffaloes engaged in plowing, harrowing, and transplanting rice seedlings, their movements synchronized with the gentle waves of the water.

The visual cues are unmistakable: the small figure of a farmer bending over beside a majestic buffalo, their actions mirroring the timeless cycle of planting and harvesting. The deep brown tones of the wooden puppets, enhanced by the glossy sơn mài coating, contrast beautifully against the shimmering water, creating a striking visual effect. The rustic bamboo screens framing the performance add to the authenticity, immersing viewers in a traditional Vietnamese village setting.

This performance is more than just an artistic spectacle; it is a celebration of the hardworking farmers who have sustained Vietnam’s agricultural traditions for centuries. Each movement of the puppets, each splash of water, tells a story of resilience and dedication. The laughter and applause from the audience reflect the joy and pride felt in witnessing these scenes, which resonate deeply with both locals and visitors alike. For tourists exploring the vibrant streets of Hanoi, the Thăng Long Water Puppet Theatre offers a unique window into the cultural heritage of Vietnam, providing a momentary escape from the urban hustle and bustle to reconnect with the country’s rural roots.
