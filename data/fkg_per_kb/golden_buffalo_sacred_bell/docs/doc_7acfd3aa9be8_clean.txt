Nestled within the sacred precincts of Phủ Tây Hồ, a peninsula that juts out into the windswept expanse of West Lake, stands the Golden Buffalo Sacred Bell, a monumental bronze bell that serves as a timeless cultural emblem of Hanoi. This bell, whose origins trace back to the era of land reclamation and nation-building, is deeply intertwined with a captivating folk legend involving the Zen Master Nguyễn Minh Không. According to the legend, Master Nguyễn Minh Không cast a large bronze bell known as the Đại Hồng Chung using black bronze, and when the bell's resounding tones echoed across the land, they reached even the farthest corners of the North. The sound was so powerful that it caused a Golden Buffalo to mistakenly believe it was hearing its mother’s call, prompting the buffalo to charge southward, trampling and sinking a vast area of land that eventually became the expansive West Lake. The buffalo then disappeared beneath the lake’s blue waters, leaving behind a legacy that imbues the bell with profound cultural significance.

Today, the Golden Buffalo Sacred Bell is not just a religious artifact but a vital link between the present and the rich tapestry of Vietnamese history and folklore. Its presence at the Kim Ngưu Temple is a testament to the enduring power of tradition and the spiritual heritage of the region. Constructed from solid bronze, the bell boasts a majestic cylindrical form that tapers elegantly towards its apex, exuding an ancient and solemn beauty. The bell’s surface, darkened by the passage of time, is complemented by a pair of white stone elephants positioned in a kneeling, submissive posture at the base of the sturdy gray stone hanger. These elephants add a unique touch of reverence and grandeur to the overall scene, which is further enhanced by the meticulously pruned bonsai canopies and lush green potted plants that surround the bell, creating a serene and harmonious setting.

The bell holds significant cultural importance, drawing locals and tourists alike during major festivals, full moons, and the first day of each lunar month. People from Hà Nội and beyond converge at the temple to pay their respects, offering incense and listening to the bell’s resonant tones, which create a festive yet tranquil atmosphere. As the sun sets over the lake, the bell’s deep, warm sounds echo across the water, evoking a sense of peace and tranquility that contrasts beautifully with the bustling city life nearby. This unique blend of natural beauty, historical significance, and spiritual resonance makes the Golden Buffalo Sacred Bell a cherished landmark in Hanoi, where visitors can experience a rare moment of serenity amidst the urban landscape.
