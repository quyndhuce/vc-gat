In the heart of the sacred Phủ Tây Hồ, a peninsula jutting out into the windy lake, the Golden Buffalo Sacred Bell stands as an enduring cultural symbol of the capital city. This majestic bronze bell, with its cylindrical structure tapering toward the top, exudes an ancient and solemn beauty, its deep gray hue a testament to the passage of time. The bell's imposing stature is further accentuated by the pair of white stone elephants in a kneeling, submissive posture at its foot, a unique and striking visual feature that adds to its mystique.

According to legend, the bell was cast by Zen Master Nguyễn Minh Không during the era of land reclamation and nation-building, its deep tones resonating all the way to the North and causing the Golden Buffalo to mistake it for its mother's call, leading it to trample and sink an entire area of land that is now the vast Hồ Tây, before disappearing forever beneath the blue waters. This legend has been passed down through the centuries, weaving a rich tapestry of cultural significance and symbolic value around the bell.

Today, the Golden Buffalo Sacred Bell is not only a revered religious artifact but also an anchor connecting the present with the thousand-year flow of legends. On every Tet holiday, full moon, or the first day of the lunar month, the people of Hà Thành and visitors from all over gather here to respectfully offer incense and listen to the echoing bell, creating a festive atmosphere that is both bustling and serene. As the sun sets, the deep, warm sound of the bell resonates across the water, and one's heart suddenly feels light, rediscovering a rare peace in the heart of the modern metropolis.

The bell's surroundings are equally impressive, with meticulously pruned bonsai canopies and lush green potted plants opening up to a breezy space right next to the vast waters of West Lake. The entire scene is a testament to the harmonious blend of nature and culture, as the ancient and the modern coexist in perfect balance. As one stands before the bell, surrounded by the tranquil beauty of the lake and the lush greenery, it is easy to feel a sense of connection to the city's rich cultural heritage and the enduring legacy of the Golden Buffalo Sacred Bell.
