Bat Trang Market is a pottery market located in Bat Trang Pottery Village, on the bank of the Red River in Hanoi. The market was opened in October 2004 on an area of more than 5,000 square metres. It was established as a place for displaying and trading Bat Trang pottery products and has also become a tourist attraction.

The market offers a wide range of ceramic and porcelain products made by local artisans. Visitors can find everyday household items such as cups, bowls and plates, as well as decorative objects, ceramic pictures, miniature rockeries and worship items. The variety of products reflects the long-standing pottery tradition of Bat Trang and provides visitors with opportunities to see and purchase locally produced ceramics.

Bat Trang Market is closely connected with the wider cultural and craft heritage of Bat Trang Pottery Village. Visitors can combine a visit to the market with a walk through the traditional village, observe pottery-making activities and take part in pottery-making experiences. The market therefore provides not only a place for shopping but also an opportunity to discover the traditional craft that has developed in Bat Trang for centuries.

Today, Bat Trang Market remains an important part of the tourism experience in the pottery village. Together with the village's traditional architecture, pottery workshops and cultural attractions, the market helps visitors experience the living craft traditions of Bat Trang and discover the distinctive ceramic products associated with the village.
