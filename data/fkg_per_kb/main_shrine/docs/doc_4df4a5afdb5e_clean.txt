As we step into the spiritual heart of the peninsula, we find ourselves at the Main Shrine, a majestic structure nestled within the renowned Phủ Tây Hồ complex. This sacred site, situated on the most revered ley line along the shore, is where the ethereal mist and the mortal realm converge, creating a sublime harmony that transcends the boundaries of time and space. According to legend, it was here that Chúa Liễu Hạnh, one of the Tứ bất tử of Vietnamese belief, descended to earth in the 17th century to open a tea house and compose poetry with Trạng bùng Phùng Khắc Khoan. This miraculous encounter gave birth to the phủ named Tây Hồ, a place dedicated to worshipping the sacred Thánh Mẫu.

As we gaze upon the Main Shrine, we are struck by its grandeur, with its "chữ Nhị" structure and soaring, curved "mái đao" roofs that seem to defy gravity. The brilliant red of the "cửa võng" painted vermilion and gilded, interspersed with the glossy brown "gỗ lim", creates a visual feast that is both captivating and awe-inspiring. The unique identifying feature of the Main Shrine is the majestic "hoành phi" "Tây Hồ Phong Nguyệt" hung majestically in the center, a symbol of the sacred and the sublime.

As we wander through the serene architectural complex, we are enveloped by the mystical agarwood smoke, which seems to carry the whispers of the past, and the compassionate protection of spiritual maternal love. The ancient "si" trees, casting protective shadows, seem to embrace the vast, windy lake surface, creating a sense of tranquility and peace. On full moon days, the first day of the lunar month, or during the third lunar month festival, the flags flutter across the sky, and pilgrims from all directions bring vibrant trays of offerings, reverently burning incense amidst the enchanting and captivating melodies of chầu văn singing.

As we step into this sacred space, we are cleansed of our worries, leaving profound peace in the soul amidst the hustle and bustle of life. The Main Shrine is a testament to the power of faith and the enduring legacy of Vietnamese culture, a place where the past and the present converge, and where the sacred and the sublime await us.
