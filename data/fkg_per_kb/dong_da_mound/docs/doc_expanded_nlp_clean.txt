Dong Da Mound (Gò Đống Đa) is located in Dong Da district, Hanoi. It is a historic battlefield memorial and park.
The site commemorates the Tay Son victory over Qing forces in 1789 under Quang Trung.
The commemorative landscape has changed over time.
The Quang Trung memorial statue and ceremonial spaces are situated within the Dong Da heritage park.
Paths and stairs organize access to the elevated commemorative area.
Characteristic physical features include monumental statue of Quang Trung, steps, trees, commemorative inscriptions and landscaped mound.
Dong Da Mound is associated with the following named places and physical sub-entities: Quang Trung monument, commemorative mound, steps, stone inscriptions.
The site is associated with annual Dong Da victory commemoration and historical education.
