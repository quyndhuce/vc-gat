In the heart of bustling Hanoi, nestled within the vast green space of Yen So Park, lies the enchanting Wonderland "Uoc Mo Xanh", a fairytale corner that transports visitors to a world of serenity and wonder. This picturesque complex, situated beside the southern gateway to the capital, was created with the vision of transforming the ecological park into a haven for recreation, camping, and outdoor experiences for young people and families. The name "Uoc Mo Xanh" not only evokes the lush greenery of the trees and the lake's surface, but also conveys the sense of a tranquil oasis amidst the city's hustle and bustle.

As one approaches the site, the eye is drawn to the clusters of colorful tents scattered under the shade of tall trees, their vibrant hues a stark contrast to the surrounding urban landscape. The vast lawns and the large lake, with its gentle ripples, dominate the scene, creating a sense of serenity and calm. The wooden miniatures and check-in areas, designed in a modern fairy-tale style, add a touch of whimsy and magic to the atmosphere.

The lakeside campsite, with its rows of white tents, is a popular spot for families and friends to gather, relax, and enjoy the scenic views. The surrounding waterside promenades and straight tree-lined paths invite strollers to take leisurely walks, while the open sky above provides a sense of freedom and expansiveness. As the sun begins to set, the atmosphere transforms, becoming peaceful, airy, and dreamy, like a gentle respite from the city's frenetic pace.

Throughout the year, Wonderland "Uoc Mo Xanh" hosts various outdoor music festivals, team-building activities, and camping nights, which attract a lively and youthful crowd. However, it is the quiet moments, when the sun dips below the horizon, that reveal the true essence of this enchanting place – a sense of living slowly amidst nature's splendor. Whether one is seeking a romantic getaway, a family outing, or a solo retreat, Wonderland "Uoc Mo Xanh" is a must-visit destination in Hanoi, offering a unique blend of recreation, relaxation, and cultural heritage.
