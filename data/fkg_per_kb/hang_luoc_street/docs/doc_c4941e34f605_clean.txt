Hang Luoc Street is located in the Old Quarter of Hanoi within Hoan Kiem District. The street stretches for 264 meters, starting from Hang Cot Street and ending at the intersection of Hang Ma, Cha Ca, and Thuoc Bac streets. Historically known as Song To Lich Street due to its proximity to the old To Lich River, it was renamed Hang Luoc because of the many shops that sold combs. Today, Hang Luoc Street is where city residents come to handle funeral and celebratory matters.

In the past, when the river no longer served as a port, the inhabitants relied on Dong Xuan Market for their livelihoods. They engaged in small-scale trading and craftsmanship. Some families were civil servants or employees of private enterprises, with husbands working in government offices while wives sold goods at the market.

The street is served by bus routes 31 and 146, which terminate at its end. Notable landmarks along Hang Luoc Street include the Quynh House and the Dinh House.
