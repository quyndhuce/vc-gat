Bell House, a term that can refer to various locations and structures around the world, encompasses a diverse array of buildings and places. In Canada, one notable example is the Bell House built in 1910 in Nutana, Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, which stands as an important residential structure. Additionally, Bellhouse Provincial Park in British Columbia adds another layer to the geographical significance of this name.

In Ireland, Bell House is associated with Cambridge House Grammar School's house system, named after the musician Derek Bell. This connection highlights the cultural importance of the name within educational institutions.

Moving to the United Kingdom, several Bell Houses are scattered across different regions. The Bell House Gallery in Buntingford, England, showcases artistic endeavors, while Bell House in Coxwold, North Yorkshire, serves as an almshouse. In Dulwich, another Bell House exists, contributing to the architectural landscape of the area. Ramsden Bellhouse is a village in Essex, and Bell House Common in Hanworth, Norfolk, further illustrates the presence of this name in various forms. Notably, Bell House in Milton of Campsie, Stirlingshire, Scotland, has been recognized as one of the 100 best modern Scottish buildings by Prospect magazine.

In the United States, the term "Bell House" is even more widespread, appearing in numerous states. In Alabama, there are several examples, including the Willie Bell House in Mason's Bend, a project by the Rural Studio, and the Bell House in Prattville. Similarly, in Jonesboro, Arkansas, there is a Bell House, alongside Lake-Bell House in Prairie Grove and another in Searcy. The James George Bell House in Los Angeles County, California, is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, highlighting its historical significance.

Delaware’s Bell Farmhouse in Newark and the R. H. and Jessie Bell House in Ada County, Idaho, are also listed on the National Register of Historic Places, emphasizing their historical value. In Iowa, the Alvin Bushnell Bell House in Ida Grove, Foster/Bell House in Ottumwa, and Hill McClelland Bell House in Des Moines are all significant structures. Kansas features the George and Annie Bell House, a historic site in Douglas County.

Kentucky has multiple Bell Houses, such as those in Edmonton, John Bell House in Lexington, and Page-Bell House in Trimble County. Maryland’s Bell-Varner House in Leitersburg and Michigan’s Bell-Spalding House in Ann Arbor are additional examples. In Mississippi, the Bell House in Starkville is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, as is the Jasper Newton Bell House in Lancaster County, Nebraska.

New York’s Mary E. Bell House in Center Moriches and The Bell House in Brooklyn, a comedy and music venue, represent the diversity of structures bearing this name. North Carolina’s Bell-Sherrod House in Enfield and Bryan-Bell Farm in Pollocksville are also noteworthy. Ohio’s Bell House in Fredericktown and Bell's First Home in Highland County are listed on the National Register of Historic Places, along with the Dr. James Bell House in Cleveland. Pennsylvania’s John C. Bell House in Philadelphia and Rhode Island’s Isaac Bell House in Newport are further examples.

Texas has several Bell Houses, including the Allen-Bell House in Bastrop County, John Y. Bell House in DeWitt County, Rogers-Bell House in Travis County, and Faires-Bell House in Collin County. Utah’s Bell-Johnson House in Cache County and Virginia’s Bell House in Colonial Beach and Howard-Bell-Feather House in Riner are also included.
