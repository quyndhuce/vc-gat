Saigon Opera House (Nhà hát Lớn TP. Hồ Chí Minh) is located in Dong Khoi Street, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City. It is a french-colonial performing-arts building.
The Municipal Theatre was constructed during the French colonial era and has served changing performance and civic functions.
The front facade addresses a central-city street and plaza.
The auditorium and stage are behind the formal entrance.
Characteristic physical features include ornate facade, sculptural decoration, arches, central entrance and auditorium.
