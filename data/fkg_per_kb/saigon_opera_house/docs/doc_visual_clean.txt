The Saigon Opera House, located on Lam Son Square along Dong Khoi Street in District 1, features an ornate Flamboyant Beaux-Arts style façade. The ivory-white stone façade includes a grand arched central portal framed by Corinthian pilasters, sculpted relief balustrades, and caryatid statues supporting a triangular pediment adorned with winged female angel figures. The roof is topped with ornate metal crests. Inside, the theatre has a main seating floor plus two levels of seating, originally capable of accommodating 1,800 people. The building also includes notable engineering details such as a raised floor approximately two meters above street level and a two-layer soundproof door system to insulate the auditorium from city noise.
