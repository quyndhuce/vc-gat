Hồ Văn Mười, born on August 10, 1969, in Thu Bồn, Danang City (formerly Duy Hòa, Duy Xuyen, Quang Nam Province), is a police officer and political figure in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. He currently serves as a member of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Vietnam's 14th term, Deputy Secretary of the Provincial Committee of Lam Dong Province, Secretary of the Party Committee, and Chairman of the People's Council of Lam Dong Province, concurrently holding administrative leadership positions in the province.

Previously, Hồ Văn Mười was Deputy Secretary of the Provincial Committee of Dak Nong Province, Secretary of the Party Committee, Chairman of the People's Council of Dak Nong Province, Secretary of the Party Committee, Director of the Dak Nong Provincial Police Department, Deputy Chief of the Financial and Economic Security Bureau under the Ministry of Public Security, and Deputy Chief of the Internal Security Bureau under the Ministry of Public Security.

Born in Thu Bồn, Đà Nẵng, he grew up and completed his secondary education there. In 1987, he moved to Hanoi to study at the University of Public Security (now the People's Security Academy), where he earned a Bachelor’s degree in Public Security. Subsequently, he obtained additional Bachelor’s degrees in Economics and State Administration, as well as a Master’s degree in Law. Hồ Văn Mười joined the Communist Party of Vietnam on December 28, 1993, becoming a full member on December 28, 1994. Throughout his career, he attended various courses at the Ho Chi Minh National Political Academy and received a Master’s degree in Political Theory. Currently, he resides in Nam Gia Nghĩa Ward, Lam Dong Province.

In July 1993, after graduating from university, Hồ Văn Mười began his career as an officer and investigator at the First Political Protection Bureau under the Ministry of Interior. In March 1993, the Third Political Protection Bureau was established within the Counterintelligence General Corps, and he transferred to become an officer and investigator there. Following the separation of the Ministry of Public Security, he joined the Security General Corps, rising to Deputy Head of the Department and Assistant Deputy Chief of the Security General Corps. In October 2006, he was reassigned to Dong Nai Province, joining the Party Committee of the Dong Nai Provincial Police Department, where he served as Secretary of the Party Branch and Head of the Economic Security Department for six years.

In September 2012, Hồ Văn Mười was transferred to Ho Chi Minh City, assuming the position of Deputy Secretary of the Party Branch and Deputy Chief of the Financial and Economic Security Bureau under the Security General Corps in Ho Chi Minh City. In September 2015, he was appointed Secretary of the Party Committee and Deputy Chief of the Fifth Political Protection Bureau under the Security General Corps. In August 2018, following organizational restructuring within the Ministry of Public Security, he was assigned as a member of the Standing Committee, Secretary of the Party Committee, and Deputy Chief of the Internal Security Bureau in Ho Chi Minh City. On December 6, 2019, Minister of Public Security To Lam reassigned him to the Dak Nong Provincial Police Department, where he assumed the role of Director of the Dak Nong Provincial Police Department.
