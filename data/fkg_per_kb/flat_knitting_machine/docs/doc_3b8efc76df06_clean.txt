Stepping into the heart of Van Phuc Ward, nestled within the bustling Ha Dong District, one is greeted by the vibrant energy of modern flat knitting machines. These machines stand as a testament to the enduring legacy of silk weaving, a tradition that traces back over a millennium to the time when Thanh Hoang A La Thi Nuong first imparted the art of weaving to her community. The flat knitting machines, with their robust and rectangular frames resembling giant tables, are a blend of industrial might and cultural heritage, seamlessly integrating into the traditional silk workshops.

The machines, constructed from sturdy alloy with a distinctive silver-gray hue, are adorned with colorful yarns in various hues, creating a mesmerizing spectacle. Their most striking feature is the slider assembly, which houses thousands of tiny needles that glide swiftly across the machine, both horizontally and vertically, producing intricate patterns with breathtaking speed. These machines are the centerpiece of the brightly lit textile workshops, where they stand proudly amidst neatly stacked rustic yarn spools, adding a touch of modernity to the age-old craft.

In these workshops, skilled artisans meticulously program and monitor the machines, ensuring each stitch is perfectly executed. Visitors are often captivated by the rapid formation of silk fabrics, marveling at the seamless integration of technology and craftsmanship. During cultural weeks dedicated to the craft village, the rhythmic hum of the machines harmonizes with the festive atmosphere, creating a symphony of labor that resonates with pride and tradition.

The flat knitting machines not only serve as functional tools but also carry profound cultural significance. They embody the aspirations of the people of Van Phuc, silently weaving the name of the village into each piece of fabric, symbolizing the wish for prosperity and well-being. This blend of past and present ensures that the traditional silk threads remain vibrant and lustrous, adapting to the changing times while preserving their timeless beauty. The machines thus represent the dynamic nature of heritage, demonstrating how it thrives through continuous transformation rather than remaining static.

These flat knitting machines are integral to the cultural landscape of Van Phuc Ward, serving as a bridge between ancient traditions and contemporary innovation. They attract tourists and enthusiasts alike, offering a glimpse into the rich tapestry of Vietnamese silk weaving, a practice that continues to thrive and evolve with each passing day.
