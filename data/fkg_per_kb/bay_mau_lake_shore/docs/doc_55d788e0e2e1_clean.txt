Bay Mau Lake Shore, nestled within the heart of bustling Hanoi, is a serene oasis that captivates visitors with its natural beauty and tranquil atmosphere. Covering approximately 28 hectares, this picturesque lake is a significant part of Thong Nhat Park, offering a refreshing escape from the city's hustle and bustle. The lake's name, "Bay Mau," translates to "Seven Mu," referring to its original size of around seven mu of land, equivalent to roughly 25,200 square meters, which gives it a unique characteristic and memorable identity.

The lake is divided into two parts by two islands, Thong Nhat Island and Hoa Binh Island, each contributing to the diverse landscape and activities available for visitors. Thong Nhat Island, home to a beautiful flower garden and connected to the park via a bridge, provides a scenic backdrop for leisurely walks and gatherings. Meanwhile, Hoa Binh Island, located closer to the eastern shore, offers a peaceful retreat where one can enjoy the cool breeze and lush greenery. Both islands are accessible by small boats, adding to the charm and accessibility of the area.

Bay Mau Lake Shore opens its gates daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, inviting tourists and locals alike to explore its serene waters and verdant surroundings. The lake's calm waters and lush vegetation create a perfect setting for various recreational activities. Early morning and late afternoon are particularly popular times for walking, cycling, and exercising around the lake. The shaded paths lined with trees provide a refreshing atmosphere, ideal for physical activity and relaxation. Couples often choose this tranquil spot for romantic strolls, while groups of friends and families gather to enjoy the fresh air and scenic views.

One of the most delightful activities at Bay Mau Lake Shore is renting a pedal boat, commonly known as a "ducks" boat, allowing visitors to glide across the water and experience the serene ambiance up close. Additionally, fishing enthusiasts can find joy in the numerous anglers who frequent the lake, creating a picturesque scene of tranquility and leisure. The lake's calm waters and abundant fish make it an ideal spot for those looking to unwind and connect with nature.

The cultural significance of Bay Mau Lake Shore is further enhanced by the presence of a statue on Thong Nhat Island, depicting Uncle Ho holding hands with Uncle Ton, celebrating his election as Vice President. This monument not only honors the contributions of these national figures but also serves as a testament to the community spirit and dedication that went into the development of the lake. Visitors are encouraged to visit this statue to appreciate its artistic value and historical importance.

Moreover, Bay Mau Lake Shore is a popular destination for team-building activities and outdoor recreation. The spacious and serene environment of Thong Nhat Island makes it an ideal location for corporate retreats, family outings, and social gatherings. Various clubs and groups organize events here, fostering a sense of community and enjoyment among participants. The island's open spaces and natural beauty provide a perfect setting for team-building exercises, outdoor games, and even camping trips.

In summary, Bay Mau Lake Shore stands out as a must-visit destination in Hanoi, offering a blend of natural beauty, recreational activities, and cultural significance. Its serene waters, lush landscapes, and historical monuments make it a haven for both tourists and locals seeking a moment of peace and relaxation amidst the vibrant city life.

Bay Mau Lake Shore, nestled within the expansive grounds of Thong Nhat Park in Hai Ba Trung district of Hanoi, stands out as a verdant oasis amidst the bustling urban landscape of the Vietnamese capital. This serene body of water, known for its significant role in the city's environmental balance, is not only a vital green lung but also a cherished destination for both locals and tourists seeking respite from the city’s hustle and bustle.

The lake, with its approximate surface area of 28.2 hectares, occupies a strategic location in the southern part of central Hanoi, bordered by major thoroughfares such as Dai Co Viet to the south, Van Ho III and Nguyen Dinh Chieu to the east and southeast, and Le Duan to the west. The proximity to these key routes makes it easily accessible, while its adjacency to Thong Nhat Park ensures that it remains a focal point for recreational activities and natural beauty.

The name "Bay Mau Lake" derives from its original size of approximately 7 mu (equivalent to 25,200 square meters), which has since expanded through various renovations and developments, particularly following the establishment of Thong Nhat Park in 1960. This expansion has transformed the lake into a crucial component of the park’s extensive green space, contributing significantly to the urban ecosystem.

Bay Mau Lake Shore serves as a sanctuary where visitors can immerse themselves in the tranquility of nature. The lake’s picturesque setting includes two charming islands, Thong Nhat Island and Hoa Binh Island, each offering unique attractions. Thong Nhat Island, connected to the park via a quaint bridge, houses a vibrant flower garden, while Hoa Binh Island provides a peaceful retreat for those desiring solitude and calmness by the water.

The lake’s verdant surroundings, complemented by the vast expanse of water, create a refreshing atmosphere that helps regulate the city’s temperature, especially during the scorching summer months. The lush vegetation around the lake, combined with the cooling effect of the water, turns this natural haven into a giant air conditioner, providing a much-needed respite from the urban heat.

Visitors to Bay Mau Lake Shore can enjoy a variety of activities that cater to different interests. Strolling along the well-paved paths lined with ancient trees, one can breathe in the fresh air and admire the serene waters of the lake. Early mornings and evenings are particularly popular times for leisurely walks or cycling around the lake, allowing individuals to engage in physical activity while enjoying the tranquil ambiance.

For photography enthusiasts, the lake offers numerous picturesque spots that serve as ideal backdrops for capturing memorable moments. The dense foliage, reflective surfaces of the water, and the small bridges leading to the islands provide endless opportunities for creating stunning images. These elements are especially enchanting during spring and autumn when the scenery takes on a more poetic and romantic quality.

In addition to sightseeing, Bay Mau Lake Shore also hosts various recreational activities. Yoga sessions and meditation classes take place in the serene environment, offering a perfect blend of physical and mental rejuvenation. Sports enthusiasts can play badminton or shuttlecock in designated areas near the lake, while anglers often gather in the evenings to enjoy a peaceful session of fishing.

One of the most delightful experiences at the lake is the opportunity to pedal a swan-shaped boat across its expansive waters. This activity allows visitors to glide smoothly over the clear waters, feeling the gentle breeze against their skin while taking in panoramic views of the entire lake. It is a favorite among couples looking for a romantic outing.

The heart of Bay Mau Lake is Thong Nhat Island, home to a historic monument dedicated to Uncle Ho and Uncle Ton, commemorating a significant event in Vietnam’s history when Uncle Ton was elected as Vice President of the country. On weekends, the street comes alive with street performances, folk games, flash mobs, and food stalls, providing a dynamic mix of cultural and recreational activities that enhance the overall experience of visiting Bay Mau Lake Shore.
