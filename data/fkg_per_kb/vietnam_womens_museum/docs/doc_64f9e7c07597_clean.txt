The Vietnam Women's Museum is located at 36 Ly Thai To Street, Hang Bai Ward, Hoan Kiem District, Hanoi, near Hoan Kiem Lake and the Old Quarter. Established in 1987 under the Vietnam Women's Union, the museum opened to the public in 1995 and has since served hundreds of thousands of domestic and international visitors annually. It regularly updates its permanent exhibitions from 2006 to 2010 to honor Vietnamese women. The museum’s main building consists of four areas: permanent exhibition space, special exhibition space, an exploration room, and a souvenir shop. Special exhibitions are organized near the main building area.

The museum houses a collection of approximately 25,000 documents and artifacts related to Vietnamese women, dating back to the 1970s. These items are categorized by materials such as textiles, metal, wood, paper, ceramics, leather, horn, clay, and glass. Each artifact tells stories about experiences during different historical periods.

Permanent exhibitions are displayed on the second, third, and fourth floors of the museum building, covering three themes: women in the family, women in history, and women's fashion. The first part, "Women in the Family" on the second floor, illustrates the life cycle of Vietnamese women through marriage customs, childbirth, and family life. The third floor's "Women in History" section highlights women's roles in national resistance wars and their contributions to contemporary society. The fourth floor's "Women's Fashion"展区介绍了越南各民族妇女的特色服饰和技艺，如苗族和傣族的刺绣、苗族使用的蜡染技术。通过展示发饰、项链、耳环、手镯、牙签等收藏品，反映了少数民族妇女的美容艺术，以及嚼食槟榔和染齿的习俗，这些都增添了越南女性的独特魅力。

除了永久展览外，博物馆还组织了许多专题展览，采用社会人类学的方法，反映当代社会的发展和变化，面向不同的群体，特别是弱势妇女和儿童。例如，“市场的故事”展览与Health Bridge和Fresh Studio合作举办（于2014年8月开幕）；“创意女性”展览是与越南妇联、世界银行和联合国妇女署合作举办的活动之一，以响应国际妇女节。
