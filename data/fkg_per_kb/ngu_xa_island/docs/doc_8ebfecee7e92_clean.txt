In the heart of bustling Hanoi lies a charming island known as Ngu Xa Island, a vibrant hub that seamlessly blends culinary delights with historical charm. The island, nestled within the picturesque Truc Bach Lake, is home to a bustling night market and pedestrian zone that has become a focal point for both locals and tourists alike. The area, officially recognized as a tourist destination by the Hanoi City People's Committee in 2024, has seen significant development aimed at enhancing its appeal and enriching the visitor experience.

The plans for the upcoming quarter of 2025 include the renovation of the streets within the night market area, specifically focusing on paving the roads with stone. This initiative, spearheaded by the Ba Dinh District People's Committee, aims to create a more aesthetically pleasing and pedestrian-friendly environment. The project is expected to enhance the overall ambiance of the island, making it even more inviting for visitors who wish to explore the unique blend of culinary offerings and cultural activities.

The night market on Ngu Xa Island operates daily, with special arrangements made during weekends to ensure a more relaxed and enjoyable atmosphere. From Friday evening at 6 PM until midnight on Sunday, vehicular traffic is restricted along Nguyen Khac Hieu and Ngu Xa streets, allowing pedestrians to freely wander and enjoy the array of food stalls and cultural performances. This setup not only promotes a safer and more pleasant walking experience but also fosters a lively community atmosphere where visitors can immerse themselves in the local culture.

Since its inception nearly two years ago, the night market has witnessed remarkable growth, with an increase of 145 new businesses joining the existing establishments. This expansion has significantly boosted revenue, marking a 245% rise compared to pre-project figures. The success of the night market is evident in the diverse range of activities offered, from traditional craft demonstrations to live cultural performances, which cater to a wide audience and provide a rich tapestry of experiences.

One of the standout attractions is the "Tuyen tau dien so 6 - Bao tang duong pho Ha Noi" (Trolley Line 6 - Street Museum of Hanoi), which was inaugurated recently. This innovative addition offers visitors a unique way to explore the island while learning about the history and culture of Hanoi through interactive exhibits and guided tours. The trolley line serves as a mobile museum, transporting passengers through various stops that highlight different aspects of the city’s heritage.

The cultural significance of Ngu Xa Island extends beyond its culinary offerings. It is home to several historical sites, including the ancient Temple of Thien Quang and the historic Ngu Xa Communal House. These landmarks serve as living museums, preserving the traditions and stories of the past. During festive periods and holidays, these sites open their doors to the public, hosting traditional ceremonies and events that showcase the region’s rich cultural heritage. Visitors can witness age-old rituals and participate in activities that offer a glimpse into the spiritual and communal life of the area.

The island’s commitment to preserving and promoting local crafts is exemplified by the establishment of the Bronze Casting Museum. Here, artisans demonstrate the intricate process of bronze casting, a skill passed down through generations. The museum not only showcases the finished products but also provides insights into the techniques and tools used in this traditional craft. This hands-on approach allows visitors to appreciate the craftsmanship and the historical context behind each piece.

In addition to the cultural and historical attractions, Ngu Xa Island is renowned for its culinary scene. The night market features a variety of street food vendors offering a range of dishes, from traditional Vietnamese delicacies to modern fusion cuisine. The combination of aromatic street food, vibrant decorations, and the sounds of live music creates a dynamic and engaging atmosphere that appeals to all senses. Visitors can sample dishes such as pho, banh mi, and other local specialties while enjoying the lively ambiance of the market.

The island’s natural beauty, complemented by the serene waters of Truc Bach Lake, adds another layer of charm to the visitor experience. The lake provides a tranquil backdrop against which the bustling activity of the night market unfolds. The juxtaposition of the serene water and the lively market creates a unique setting that is both calming and invigorating.

Overall, Ngu Xa Island stands out as a destination that seamlessly integrates tradition with modernity, offering visitors a multifaceted experience that is both educational and entertaining. Whether one is interested in exploring historical sites, sampling local cuisine, or simply enjoying the vibrant atmosphere, Ngu Xa Island promises a memorable visit filled with rich cultural encounters and delightful surprises.

Ngũ Xã Island, a vibrant and culturally rich area within the bustling city of Hà Nội, stands out as a unique destination for tourists seeking a blend of culinary delights and historical charm. The island, also known as Đảo Ngọc - Ngũ Xã, has become a focal point for both locals and visitors, offering a distinctive night-time food market that combines gastronomy with cultural heritage. This area, nestled in the heart of the Trúc Bạch district, is set to undergo significant enhancements aimed at further enriching the visitor experience.

Since its inception nearly two years ago, the night-time food market on Ngũ Xã Island has seen remarkable growth, with the number of businesses increasing by 145 establishments. This surge in commercial activity has not only boosted economic activity but also enhanced the cultural vibrancy of the region. The revenue generated from these businesses has skyrocketed by 245%, reflecting the growing popularity and appeal of the area.

The island's development is part of a broader initiative to promote Hà Nội as a safe, friendly, and high-quality tourist destination. It aims to highlight the traditional crafts and local delicacies that are integral to the city’s identity. One such craft is the renowned bronze casting tradition, which is celebrated and showcased through various events and exhibitions. These activities serve to educate visitors about the historical significance and intricate processes involved in this ancient art form.
