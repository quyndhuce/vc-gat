Nestled within the heart of Hanoi's Ba Dinh District, Ngu Xa Island, also known as Dao Ngoc Ngu Xa, is a picturesque crescent-shaped strip of land that floats serenely on the sparkling waters of Truc Bach Lake. This small yet enchanting island, which lies adjacent to the tranquil lake, serves as a unique blend of historical charm and vibrant modernity, offering visitors a serene escape from the bustling city life.

The origins of Ngu Xa Island date back to the seventeenth century when skilled artisans from five renowned bronze casting villages gathered here to establish their livelihoods. These talented craftsmen, who were instrumental in preserving the ancient Thang Long bronze casting tradition, gave the island its name, Ngũ Xa, symbolizing the unity and excellence of these artisan communities. The island thus became a testament to the rich cultural heritage of Hanoi, embodying the legacy of these master artisans.

Today, the island has transformed into a bustling culinary haven, where the pace of life has shifted from the quiet craftsmanship of old to a lively scene of gastronomic delights. As dusk falls, the streets of Ngu Xa Island come alive with the sounds of laughter and chatter as visitors from all corners of the city and beyond converge upon the pedestrian street. Here, they savor the famous local dishes such as pho cuon and pho chien phong, served in the many closely packed eateries that line the walkways. The sight of these inviting plates of pho cuon displayed prominently along the pathways adds to the island's distinctive charm, drawing in food enthusiasts and casual strollers alike.

The visual appeal of Ngu Xa Island is enhanced by its striking architectural features and the interplay of light and color. The gray tones of the neighborhood, punctuated by brilliant yellow lights that illuminate the eateries at night, create a warm and welcoming ambiance. This contrast between the cool gray hues and the warm golden lights reflects the harmonious coexistence of traditional and contemporary elements, making the island a captivating destination for both locals and tourists.

In addition to its culinary offerings, Ngu Xa Island stands out as a significant cultural landmark within Hanoi’s urban landscape. It not only honors the proud legacy of the ancient artisans but also serves as a soothing retreat for weary travelers seeking respite from the hustle and bustle of the city. The island’s serene setting, combined with its vibrant culinary scene, makes it a perfect blend of historical significance and modern vibrancy, ensuring that visitors leave with a memorable experience that encapsulates the essence of Hanoi’s rich cultural tapestry.
