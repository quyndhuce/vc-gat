Set aside the hustle and bustle of the city and immerse yourself in the enchanting performance "Children Playing with Water" at the Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre, a cultural gem located on Dinh Tien Hoang Street, adjacent to the serene shores of Hoan Kiem Lake in the heart of Hanoi. Established in 1969, this theatrical piece has been a beacon of pride for the Thang Long name, which dates back to the time when King Ly Thai To moved the capital to Thang Long, now known as Hanoi. The enduring vitality of this art form, born from the heart of Vietnam’s wet rice civilization, is a testament to its timeless appeal and cultural significance.

The performance begins with the melodic sounds of the đàn bầu, a traditional Vietnamese instrument, blending seamlessly with the rhythmic clapping of wooden sticks. These sounds transform the entire theatre into a vibrant riverside wharf, evoking the lively atmosphere of a rural summer day. Spectators from far and wide, along with local residents, are captivated by the sight of bare-chested children frolicking and diving in the water, their movements synchronized with the puppeteers’ skilled manipulation. The small, plump figurines of the swimming children, crafted from glossy lacquered wood, draw immediate attention with their rosy skin tones and dynamic dives that create splashes of white foam.

As the water surface gradually calms, the audience is left with a profound sense of peace and a nostalgic longing for childhood. This performance not only showcases the pinnacle of puppetry control but also serves as a magical journey, transporting viewers back to their innocent memories amidst the bustling city. The rustic bamboo screen hanging behind the performers adds to the authenticity of the scene, creating a vivid depiction of a traditional riverside setting.

The Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre, a significant cultural landmark, stands as a testament to the rich heritage of Vietnam. Its performances, such as "Children Playing with Water," continue to attract visitors from around the world, offering a unique glimpse into the country’s artistic traditions and cultural identity.
